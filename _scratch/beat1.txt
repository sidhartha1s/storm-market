Sakurajima is erupting again this week. The volcano in Kagoshima Bay, Kyushu, has thrown ash more than two thousand five hundred meters above its summit crater. Japan's meteorological agency counts six separate eruptions from that crater in four days. Alert level three stays in force over Kagoshima city. A two kilometer zone around the crater stays closed to entry. As of this check on September fifteenth, the most current confirmed bulletin still covers activity through September fourteenth. No new eruption, advisory, or ashfall forecast dated today has surfaced anywhere. The volcano's pattern from that last bulletin has not changed.
