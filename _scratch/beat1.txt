Sakurajima is erupting again this week. The volcano in Kagoshima Bay, Kyushu, has thrown ash more than two thousand five hundred meters above its summit crater. Japan's meteorological agency counts six separate eruptions from that crater in four days, while volcanic earthquakes fall across the same span. Alert level three stays in force over Kagoshima city. A two kilometer zone around the crater stays closed to entry, as it has for years. As of this check on September fifteenth, the most current confirmed bulletin still covers activity through September fourteenth. No new eruption, advisory, or ashfall forecast dated today has surfaced anywhere. The volcano's pattern from that last confirmed bulletin has not changed.
