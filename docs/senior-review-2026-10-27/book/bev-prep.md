## 4. My tornado warning plan (weekday afternoon, kids here)
"The book says a watch is when we leave, not the warning, because by the warning the drive is the danger. I will not do that. A watch happens all spring; with two kids and a husband who takes four minutes to reach the car, I am not leaving every time. So on a watch I keep the TV on, keep everyone home, and the tote stays by the door.

When the radio goes off or my phone screams or the station says Moore is in the warning, I do not look outside. That is the Joplin thing. Helmets on the kids, Ray into the car, leash on Peanut, grab the tote, drive to the church. Eight minutes with no traffic, but I never timed it or practiced it, and I do not know if the door is unlocked.

If the church is locked, I have no second place. The book says the trailer is not survivable and gives me nowhere else. I would think of the neighbor's block garage, but I never asked them.

Peanut goes on my lap. The book says ninety seconds to the room; with Ray's cane and a 4-year-old, that is not happening."

## 5. Where the book lost me
- "you accept that you will do this several times for nothing" (P4-C2-places.md): a trip every watch, with gas and a work shift. Unrealistic.
- "Identify the place, confirm it is accessible at night." (P3-01-tornado.md): how, who do I call, what if nobody answers?
- "Tornado Alley rank 8. Owners only." (P2-C7-structure-hardening.md): do not know what rank means.
- "Protection 112" and "(P43)" cross-references everywhere (P0-front-matter.md): I flip pages to learn what 25 means.
- "SAME programming" (P5-C1-warning-literacy.md): I needed the next paragraph to understand it.
- "forty-five of the one hundred and thirty protections" (P7-the-60-days.md): I wanted "here are my three."
- "Form 12 · The three rooms" (P6-the-binder.md): I have a bedroom, a bath and a living room.
- The «SRC» blocks and "What this does not do" paragraphs: I skimmed all of them.

## 6. Where I needed a picture
- A one-page "I live in a mobile home" card: watch, warning, where to go, with times.
- A drawing of what a trailer does in EF-1 wind, so I believe it.

## 7. What the book never told me
- Where to go in a single-wide when the shelter is locked.
- How a slow husband with a cane, a 4-year-old and a dog get to a car in ten minutes.
- What happens when the warning hits while I am at the register and the kids are at school.
- A "first $50" list for someone on $2,800 a month with debt.
- How to ask the park owner about a shelter or tie-downs.
- Whether sitting in a parked car beats staying home.
- What to do with Peanut at a community shelter that excludes pets.
