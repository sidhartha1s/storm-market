## The three open markers
1. "That Christmas" scope (H1-S1): CONFIRMED. AP names the storm "Christmas week"; the NYU report covers the whole blizzard response, and the unanswered calls were part of it (backlog of 1,100 calls during the storm, RS). No page dates an individual unanswered call, so the scope is the storm, not exact days. The line says "some", not "seven", and gives no date for the calls: keep it that way.
2. "At last count" (H1-S2): CONFIRMED. WIVB states three more cases were yet to be finalized, so the count was not closed; "at last count" is the correct hedge, same as "last reported count" in the source line. Limit: the WIVB page carries no absolute date and no later count was found, so never say "final".
3. Twelve "no power or heat" at home (H1-S2): NOT SETTLED, so CORRECTED to the sourced wording. The final WIVB category says only "no power or heat". Only the 30 Dec WBFO count (11) said "inside unheated homes" (D). Cut "at home"; compare to the category as stated. The chip must change with it.
