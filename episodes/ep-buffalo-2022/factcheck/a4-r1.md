# A4 fact-check r1 · 28 Oct 26
Sources: NYU Wagner "Lessons Learned from the Buffalo Blizzard" PDF itself (https://wagner.nyu.edu/files/faculty/publications/NYU%20Buffalo%20Blizzard%20Report%20-%20June2023_0.pdf , needs a browser User-Agent; read as full text, PRIMARY); DVIDS 436223 and 436463 (PRIMARY); dutchessny.gov 44052 (PRIMARY); CBS Miami page (SECONDARY); WIVB (SECONDARY); btpm.org/WBFO (SECONDARY). ## Findings that matter
1. S5 Dutchess dates are wrong in the script and in research (research says Fri 23 to Mon 26 Dec). The page is posted Thursday 29 Dec 2022 and says coverage runs "Friday through Monday", so 30 Dec to 2 Jan, the weekend after Christmas. "All of Christmas weekend" must go. Also "nine" dispatchers is unsupported.
2. S2 "Two days later": CBS says Poloncarz spoke "on Monday" (page updated 28 Dec), so Monday 26 Dec, the SAME day as Hochul. Research dated it 28 Dec.
3. S9 "seven ... unanswered because of the backlog" overstates. NYU p.13: seven died "because of what officials have characterized as a delayed EMS response due to backlogged calls, unplowed streets, and stranded vehicles".
4. S7 "drove her": DVIDS names no driver; Waldman and his mother's boyfriend went in a truck, then "they made their way to" ECMC. "young": no age given.
5. S10 "plenty of people did exactly that": no source. btpm gives 11 of 39 deaths inside unheated homes, which is not the same claim. Poloncarz quote date (29 Dec) not confirmed.
6. NYU report itself CONFIRMS: 22.72 h, 25 calls, 1,100 backlog, most BFD vehicles stuck. The 25-call limit is the County's software.
