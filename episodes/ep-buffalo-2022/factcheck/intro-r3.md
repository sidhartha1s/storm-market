## The four open markers
1. H1-S2 "Twelve of them were without power or heat": CONFIRMED. WIVB "12 died from no power or heat"; the line says without power or heat, not "at home", and rides on "lost their lives" in sentence 1.
2. I1-S2 "which means the snow kept blowing across the roads" and "That's why hundreds of drivers ended up stranded": first half CONFIRMED (inference, sourced); second half CORRECTED. The governor release names the storm, not wind or blowing snow, as what left motorists stranded, and AP/NYU name a travel ban "inadequately conveyed" and drivers "unaware of or ignored" it. "That's why" makes the gusts the single cause and cuts across the episode's own question (why were people out). Attribute the strandings to the governor's office and drop the causal link.
3. I1-S2 "didn't let up for thirty-seven straight hours": CORRECTED. The 37 hours is DHSES "blizzard-like conditions", not that the snow or wind never eased. And "a day and a half stuck out in it" now reads as if people were stuck out for the whole span (R2 scoped it with "if you were caught out in it"; the round-3 wording lost that scope).
4. I1-S4 "Buffalo's weather service was already calling it a very large and powerful storm system": CORRECTED. The 18 Dec AFD says a very large and powerful system "will impact the bulk of the eastern half of the country later in the week" and, for Buffalo, only "some strong winds and more accumulating snow". It did not call the Buffalo blizzard that, and it did not forecast a blizzard. Say what it wrote about the system, scoped to the eastern half of the country.
