Same line in WSWBUF 221628 (11:28 AM Thu). The 6:48 PM Thu product (222348) has the frostbite line but its preparedness text ends after "Travel is strongly discouraged." with NO stay-with-vehicle line.

CNN via WSB-TV also says 10 houses. Research's "more than a dozen" (search snippet) is WRONG.

- governor.ny.gov 26 Dec transcript: surgeon described as "head of surgery at ECMC" (per fetch summary, not a verbatim quote: re-read before using the title), rescued by State Fire teams.
