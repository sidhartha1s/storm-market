## A3 · Outside
Chapter card: Purpose · pay loop E's first half: who the people found outside were, and why they were out there, through one sourced life (Anndel Taylor) and the record's own advice.
Question · The ban was on and the warning had said what to do, so how did so many people end up outside in it?
Reveal · Drivers were stranded across the county; the warning said stay with your vehicle, and Anndel Taylor did, six minutes from home; others left their cars, and the airport logged wind chills below minus ten for more than a day, so both choices had a limit.
Visual event · a map pin six minutes from a front door; the 27 Dec tally bar with "outside" over half; a field of snow-buried cars; the warning text highlighting "stay with your vehicle"; a wind-chill line falling below zero.
Exit hook · Everyone stuck out there needed someone to come, so why couldn't anyone get to them? (Paul, Santa hat, tease into the CTA.)
