## A4 · Nobody could come
Chapter card: Purpose · pay the cta-hinge and the second half of loop E's "why nobody came": the plows, ambulances and fire trucks were stuck in the same drifts, the 911 calls backed up, and help came late and from far away.
Question · If the trucks sent to reach people were buried too, who was answering the calls, and how long did people wait?
Reveal · About two-thirds of the snow-clearing equipment got stuck, most Buffalo fire vehicles got stuck, eleven hundred emergency calls backed up, and the Guard grew from fifty-four on Christmas Eve to eight hundred forty-nine; for seven people in Erie County the help came too late.
Visual event · Guard engineers digging out an ambulance (DVIDS); a dispatch screen filling to 25 calls; a Guard headcount bar climbing from 54 to 701; a door-counter ticking to 3,755; three pins for the last three confirmed (Cheektowaga, Amherst, Buffalo).
Exit hook · The people who stayed home, as the warning asked, were behind doors nobody had reached yet: what happened inside?
