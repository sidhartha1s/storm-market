## 3. Test of the claim "17 of the first 31 outdoors, 3 in vehicles"

CONFIRMED as reported (SECONDARY): 17 outside, 3 in a vehicle, of 31, per county health department, 27 Dec evening (kaxe.org / NPR URL above). Caveat: 31 was Tuesday-evening count; 17 stayed constant to 39, so "most of the dead outdoors" is only a plurality: 17/31 = 55%, 17/39 = 44%, 18/46 = 39%. Outdoors is the single largest category at every count, but at the final count it is under half. Indoor causes (no heat 12 + home-related) plus EMS delay 7 = 19 exceed 18 outdoors. Vehicles add 4 (outdoors + vehicles = 22 of 46 = 48%).
Script-safe phrasing: "the largest single group", not "most".
