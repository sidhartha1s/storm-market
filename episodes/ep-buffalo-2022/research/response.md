Findings from NYU report (SECONDARY, via AP at https://www.whec.com/?p=297465, 2 Jun 2023 and Spectrum at the 2 Jun 2023 URL above):
- Ban timing: Hochul's policy of "more local decision-making" on road closures "likely led to a 'wait-and-see' approach" before the blizzard. (Spectrum quote; AP phrase "wait-and-see approach leading up to the blizzard".)
- Ban communication: travel bans were "inadequately conveyed"; some stranded drivers "were either unaware of or ignored" them (AP).
- Blocked routes: stranded drivers left routes blocked for plows and emergency vehicles (AP).
- Holiday: "an unprecedented storm that occurred over a holiday" (Spectrum).
- Deaths: 31 city residents died; 46 in Erie County; Black residents about two-thirds of city deaths (AP).
- Seven Erie County deaths tied to unanswered emergency calls (AP).
- Alerts: 16 percent of city residents enrolled in BUFFALERT (CTV copy of AP via search summary, SNIPPET).
- Brown response: report commissioned to "better prepare for future blizzards of this magnitude" (SNIPPET).
NYS Guidehouse: recommendations only confirmed (12, in four groups: communications, technology, capacity, coordination; includes "Clearly communicate the risks associated with weather events") from the DHSES release. Release stats: blizzard-like conditions 37 straight hours in Erie County; more than 80,000 households lost power at peak (PRIMARY, FETCHED).
