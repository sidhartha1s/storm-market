The wind led because of where Buffalo sits. By Wednesday afternoon, the discussion said the low would likely set low pressure records once it passed north of the border into Ontario. It said the southwest winds would be strongest on Lake Erie, straight down the length of the lake. Buffalo sits right at the end of it. The lake was almost entirely open water that week, so every mile let the wind pick up more moisture. The forecasters could see all of that days out.
