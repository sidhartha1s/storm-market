Then, at three fifty-four on Thursday morning, came the Blizzard Warning, in effect from seven o'clock Friday morning. That's twenty-seven hours of notice. And seven on the last Friday before Christmas is when a lot of people are pulling out of the driveway for work. The warning called for one to three feet of snow. Gusts could reach seventy miles an hour, and the wind would blow that snow around, "frequently reducing visibilities to zero."
