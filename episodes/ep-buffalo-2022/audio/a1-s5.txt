That Tuesday note had also said watches would likely go out soon. They did. At two twenty-two that same afternoon, the office put Erie County under a Winter Storm Watch. It ran from Friday morning to Monday morning. That was about two days and seventeen hours before the storm. The watch said gusts could reach sixty-five miles an hour Friday into Friday night. It said blowing snow could bring whiteouts and localized blizzard conditions. If you lived in Erie County, that watch named you. Of everything in it, I keep coming back to whiteouts, because that is what a driver would actually meet.
