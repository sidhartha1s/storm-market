Erie County's ban came from County Executive Mark Poloncarz, and it took effect at nine thirty. By then the airport had been logging gusts near sixty miles an hour for almost an hour and a half. One local station's live blog posted the ban at ten to nine. That left about forty minutes between the post and the ban. Poloncarz said later that officials believed the snow band would not reach the county until ten. They waited, in part, so workers could get home before the temperature dropped below freezing. But the airport had dropped below freezing around eight fifteen. For the people who'd left at seven, the order landed while some of them were still on the road.
