So who told them to stay off the roads? The officials were running on their own clock. Governor Kathy Hochul had declared a statewide State of Emergency on Thursday. It took effect at six on Friday morning, while it was still raining. The state had announced that commercial trucks would be banned from the Thruway starting at that same hour. It also said highway crews were preparing to close some Erie County expressways at six. But the emergency order itself set no driving ban for Erie County. Decisions like that were left to local leaders. A review the City of Buffalo commissioned later said that leaning on local decisions likely led to a wait and see approach.
