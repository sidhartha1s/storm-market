Two days before Christmas Eve 2022, the Weather Service in Buffalo wrote "blizzard warning, starting Friday morning", and they were right about almost all of it. Forty-six people in Erie County still lost their lives. The biggest single group, eighteen, were found outside, in the snow; four more in their cars; twelve at home without power or heat. This film follows Friday morning hour by hour: the forecast that held for days, the whiteout that reached the airport at 8:39, the county driving ban that took effect fifty-one minutes later, the plows and ambulances stuck in the same drifts as the people they were sent for, and why the street cost more lives than any house.
