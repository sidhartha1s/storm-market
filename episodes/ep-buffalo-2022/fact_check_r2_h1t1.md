1. "by the county's own count": breakdown is the County Executive's, via WIVB. Fine as is because the on-screen source line names him. Exact option: "by the county executive's count". Keep "last reported", never "final" (three cases pending).
2. "the week of Christmas": CONFIRMED. Warning issued Thu 22 Dec, effective Fri 23 Dec, Christmas Sun 25 Dec.
3. "once in a generation": paraphrase is fair ("could call it" keeps the hedge). Keep the card verbatim. No change.
4. "why nobody could get to them in time": CORRECTED. Not supported for the people found outside as a group. Use "why help could not reach some of them in time".
