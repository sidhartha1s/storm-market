Here is the mechanism. Along the fire's western edge, cool river air met superheated air off the burn, and the boundary between them began to turn. A lazy, invisible spin, close to the ground. Harmless, on any other day. Above it, the fire cloud was inhaling. A column of rising air, accelerating upward, right over that spin. A rising column does to spinning air what a skater does when she pulls in her arms. It stretches it. It tightens it. It speeds it up.
