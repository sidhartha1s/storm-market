The tornado crossed the city at a walking city's pace, six miles in twenty minutes, and near its widest it swallowed the Range Line Road retail corridor whole. At the Home Depot, the roof came off first, and that mattered, because the walls were made of tilt-up concrete panels, each one weighing one hundred thousand pounds, and tilt-up walls need their roof the way a card tower needs its top card. Sixty-three of the seventy-three panels came down. Where they fell inward, seven people died. In one corner of the store, the panels happened to fall outward instead, and the thirty people sheltering behind them lived.
