The investigators also found a mismatch hiding in plain sight. Weather service warnings are drawn as tight polygons around a storm's actual path. But Joplin's sirens fired city wide, all or nothing. So most residents had heard sirens, again and again, for storms that were never coming near their street. Every one of those blasts was technically correct, and every one of them taught the same quiet lesson. Sirens do not mean you. Until the evening when they did.
