The numbers are how a country remembers a disaster. But Joplin remembers people. It remembers fifty-three worshippers at Harmony Heights Baptist Church, who had no basement, and who folded into the interior hallways as the building came down over them. Fifty survived. Three women did not. One of them, Ramona Bridgeford, had come that evening for one reason. Her son was singing, and she wanted to hear him.
