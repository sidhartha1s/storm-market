At five seventeen, the National Weather Service in Springfield issued a second warning, and this one was different. This one was for the merged supercell bearing down on Joplin itself, and it came seventeen minutes before the tornado touched the ground, and twenty-four minutes before it reached the heart of the city. Let that number sit for a moment. Twenty-four minutes. By every standard the weather service had, that is a good warning. That is time to get to a basement, time to leave a parking lot, time to gather your children. The meteorology did its job that evening. What failed was everything after.
