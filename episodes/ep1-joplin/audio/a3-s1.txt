At five thirty-four, on the western edge of the city, the funnel touched grass. It was small at first, the way they always are. Within four minutes it crossed Schifferdecker Avenue into the city limits, and somewhere over those neighborhoods it did something radar would only confirm after the fact. It became an E F five, the highest rating that exists, with winds beyond two hundred miles per hour. Faster than a Formula One car at full speed, and nearly a mile wide.
