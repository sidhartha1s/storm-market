At five forty-one it reached Saint John's Regional Medical Center, nine stories of concrete and glass, with one hundred eighty-three patients inside. The tornado hit the building directly. It tore off the top two floors, blew out nearly every window, and pushed the entire hospital four inches off its foundation. Four inches does not sound like much, until you remember it is a hospital, and it weighs more than a naval ship. The main power failed, and then the backup generator was ripped out of the building itself, and five patients on ventilators died in the dark.
