And it remembers Will Norton, eighteen years old, four hours a high school graduate. He was driving home from the ceremony with his father when the tornado crossed the highway and took the car. His father survived. Will did not. He had already been accepted to film school in California, and his camera, and his easy laugh, had made him a small celebrity online long before the storm made him a name the whole country knew. He wanted to tell stories for a living. So the least we can do is tell his.
