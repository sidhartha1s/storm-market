At five oh nine, the first tornado warning went out, and it was not for the storm that would kill Joplin. It was for a different cell, north of town, that would pass with little harm. Two minutes later the sirens sounded anyway, a three minute wail across the whole city. People looked up, and looked outside, and saw grey sky and rain, and went back to their dinners. Which, given everything the sirens had taught them, was a completely reasonable thing to do. It was also the worst possible rehearsal for what came next.
