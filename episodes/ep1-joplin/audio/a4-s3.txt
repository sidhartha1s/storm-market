Out of one hundred sixty-one deaths came forty-seven findings and sixteen recommendations, and one change you have already heard, whether you know it or not. After Joplin, the weather service rewrote the language of fear itself. Warnings today are impact based. They no longer just name the storm, they name what it will do to you. Catastrophic. Mass devastation. Unsurvivable if you are above ground. That language was born in Joplin, paid for in Joplin, and it has been saving lives in every tornado warning since.
