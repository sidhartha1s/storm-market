There was one more change, smaller and closer to home. Joplin rebuilt its siren policy from the ground up. Activations became zoned and specific, the criteria were tightened, and the practice of sounding the same tone for everything, everywhere, ended. It is the kind of fix that never makes the news, because its success is measured in sirens that do not sound. But the next time Joplin hears that wail, it will mean exactly one thing. And everyone will know what it is.
