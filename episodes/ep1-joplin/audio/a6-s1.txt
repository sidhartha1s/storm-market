Joplin was not a failure of forecasting. The watch was right, the warning was early, and the radar, for all its distance, found the storm in time. What failed was softer than science. It was the space between hearing a warning and believing it. Every siren you have ever ignored has made a small bet that this time is like last time. Joplin is what it looks like when that bet finally loses. And that is why the warnings changed, so the believing could come easier.
