Then, at five thirty-eight, the sirens sounded a second time, and this is the detail that broke the system. Joplin had no all clear signal. The same tone meant everything. So when the wail rose again, twenty-seven minutes after the first one had stopped, thousands of people made a perfectly logical mistake. They heard it as the end of the earlier threat, not the arrival of a new one. The second siren, the one that meant run, now, was heard by many as permission to relax.
