And inside that grey wall, the storm was hiding one more cruelty. This was a multiple vortex tornado. Smaller twisters, two to seven of them at a time, orbited inside the main funnel, spinning up to one hundred miles per hour faster than the parent circulation. That is why the damage would look so random afterward. One house shaved to its slab, and the house next door still holding its roof. Nothing about it was random. It was geometry, moving at two hundred miles per hour.
