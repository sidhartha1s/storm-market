Here are the numbers the government's own records settled on. Over four days, more than three hundred and sixty tornadoes struck the South, the largest outbreak ever recorded. Three hundred and twenty-four people were killed by the tornadoes themselves. On April twenty-seventh alone, three hundred and sixteen people died, two hundred and thirty-four of them in Alabama. The damage passed ten billion dollars. You will find different numbers in other tellings of this day, and they disagree with each other. These come from the National Weather Service's official assessment, written by the people whose job was to understand exactly what happened. And what happened is not that the warnings failed.
