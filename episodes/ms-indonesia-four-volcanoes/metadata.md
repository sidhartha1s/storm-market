## Description (paste AFTER the channel's templated CTA block, which is already in YouTube settings)
Eight people, five of them journalists, have been missing off Anak Krakatau since their boat set out on September 7 to film the
eruption, and Indonesia's search and rescue agency, Basarnas, says today's day-seven results decide whether the search is extended
or closed. The volcano itself logged no eruption today but stays at alert level three, while Mount Ibu and Mount Semeru kept
erupting and PVMBG's head has said on the record that the six eruptions of August 31 had no shared trigger. This update also
carries last week's numbers for context: the 25-hour lava fountaining, ash near 15 km, eight closed airports and almost 3,000
flights, plus the 2018 flank collapse that killed 426 people with no earthquake to warn anyone, and why the same physics matters
for the Cascades.
