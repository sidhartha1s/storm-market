Cool, dry and seasonal early December weather will prevail across
the Midsouth through midweek, following this morning's cold 
frontal passage. Strong northerly winds will lighten overnight, 
but probably not go completely calm, as surface high pressure 
settles into Illinois. These light winds and dry air (lower to mid
20s dewpoints), should preclude fog/freezing fog formation late 
tonight. 

Fast zonal flow aloft will prevail until a weak upper trof passage
early Wednesday morning. This feature should pass through the 
area dry, given the lack of return flow moisture in the Midsouth. 
Main impact would be an increase in midlevel clouds.

Zonal flow an low amplitude ridging will prevail Thursday and
Friday, with strengthening low level return flow. Milder overnight
lows will accompany increased surface dewpoints. A few showers
will also be possible, aided by warm advection.

A progressive longwave trof remains on track to pass from the
plains into the MS River valley on Saturday. This feature will
bring our next appreciable rain chances, and perhaps a few strong
storms. Dry conditions should for Sunday, with upstream ridging 
set to move into the Midsouth early next week.

.PREV UPDATE... /issued 921 PM CST Sun Dec 5 2021/ 

Updated to include a Severe Thunderstorm watch for portions of
northeast Arkansas, the Missouri Bootheel and far northwest
Tennessee. A line of thunderstorms has recently developed along a
cold front stretching from northwest Oklahoma into southeast
Missouri. Ample moisture in in place featuring PW's around 1.3
inches and dew points in the low to middle 60s and resulting in 
CAPE in the 1000 to 1500 range. 0-6kt shear of 40-50kt to our 
west should gradually shift across northern portions of the 
Midsouth over the next several hours. Supercellular or quasi-
supercellular thunderstorms may result in damaging wind with a 
slightly less threat of hail. 0-1 km shear between 225 and 
250m^2/s^2 on the LZK 00z sounding suggest tornadoes cannot be 
ruled out. Tornadoes would be most likely with any storms that 
develop out ahead of the main line. 

Additional watches may be needed farther south later tonight. 

30

&& 

.DISCUSSION... /issued 603 PM CST Sun Dec 5 2021/ 

Showers and a few storms have been ongoing this afternoon across
the Mid South. We expect a lull in the action for the next few
hours before a cold front starts to move into the region later on
tonight. The Storm Prediction Center continues to carry a Slight
Risk for severe weather tonight into Monday morning for a large
portion of the forecast area. We expect showers and storms to
develop sometime after 10 PM this evening along that front as it
moves south. The biggest question still remains is if any
convection will really take advantage of the kinematics that this
system would provide if storms can become surface based. Looking
at the soundings this afternoon an inversion is still being shown
for portions of the area as the best convection moves through.
That's not to say that this will be a non event, but there is a
conditional component to this event. The shear will not be a
problem and helicity will begin to pile up along the front as
well...embedded within the convection. This would point to a QLCS
type event with bowing lines and segments that could produce 
damaging winds and a few tornadoes if it is able to take advantage
of the atmosphere in place. The best chances to see severe 
weather look to be mainly north of I-40 and as the system moves in
the profiles become less favorable for deeper convection. The 
good news is that this system should be out of the area after 10 
AM...and we will clear out for the afternoon and evening hours. 
The active pattern continues through the week. The next impulse 
looks to move through by mid week. As of now it looks like most of
the precip will be off to the south and east of the area. Have 
maintained some light POPs during that time frame as well. 
Southwesterly flow aloft will develop late in the week, with 
deeper moisture return and warm advection showers possible by 
Friday...and another system bringing the chance of some 
thunderstorms again by Saturday.
