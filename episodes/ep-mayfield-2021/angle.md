## Angle (locked by the owner, 21 Oct 26: ticked "Make it" on the devq card)

The owner ticked "Make it" on the Mayfield 2021 card of the three-documentaries devq page (21 Oct 26). The card came out of a two-agent debate over topics and angles; the coordinator session relayed the lock and the spine wording below to this session. The spine sentence is the card's wording, not the owner's own words.

Claim: The forecast was right days ahead, and nobody had told big workplaces what to do. On 10 December 2021 the outbreak took 89 lives directly with 71 tornadoes across 8 states, the deadliest December on record since 1950, and people lost their lives at work: 6 at the Amazon delivery station in Edwardsville, Illinois, and 8 at the Mayfield Consumer Products candle factory in Kentucky. The Weather Service's own assessment found it had no guidance for buildings that size.
Spine / arcs: Spine = the forecast against the workplace. Memphis's forecast discussion five days out (6 Dec) through the Storm Prediction Center outlooks and the warnings that night, set against the two large-footprint buildings where people were still at work. Arc 1 = Edwardsville (the first workplace, earlier in the evening). Arc 2 = the candle factory in Mayfield, on the 165.6 mile track. Close = Service Assessment Finding 6: no NWS guidance for large-footprint buildings.
Source: NWS Service Assessment, The Historic December 10-11, 2021 Tornado Outbreak (p4 toll, p14 the 6 Dec Memphis discussion, p34 Finding 6); NWS Paducah event page; OSHA and the Kentucky Labor Cabinet for the workplace findings.
Ruled out as the lead: the long-track EF4 itself (path length, 190 mph, "the Quad-State tornado"); the live stream from under the factory rubble; the courthouse and downtown Mayfield destroyed; the December rarity on its own. Every top video leads with one of them (teardowns: `research/teardowns.md`).
Still unverified: the Amazon and candle-plant death split; which agency cited the candle plant and the amount (Hop Town Chronicle 22 Aug 2022: OSHA, 7 violations, $40,000, contested; a 2019 Kentucky Labor Cabinet $9,810 settlement is a separate matter); the wording of the 6 Dec Memphis discussion; the 165.6 mile track length.

## Legal care that rides with the angle (coordinator brief, 21 Oct 26)
- Script it as "no guidance existed for large buildings", never as blaming a company.
- Claims that workers were threatened with firing are allegations only: attributed to who made them, or left out.
