## What the fact check changed (21 Oct 26; wording, not meaning, so the lock stands; `research/fact-sheet.md`)
- "Five days" is four: the Memphis discussion's first issuance with the line is 3:36 PM CST Monday 6 Dec (SA p14, IEM archive). The Storm Prediction Center first named that Friday a week ahead, on Day 8 (3 Dec, SA p7), without drawing a risk area.
- "Deadliest December" is "the deadliest December tornado event on record since 1950" (SA p4).
- "Eight states" is cut: SA p4 says eight, its own Appendix C lists ten state codes.
- Finding 6 says "a gap in NWS safety outreach and resources related to buildings with large footprints"; the "no guidance" words are the WFO St. Louis after-action review, quoted in the SA p33-34. Attribute each to its own source.
- Amazon's six were contractors working at the building, not Amazon staff (OSHA). The candle plant was cited by Kentucky's state OSHA program: 7 serious violations, $40,000 proposed, all contested, none about a shelter. The 2019 $9,810 settlement is a separate matter. Confirmed: 165.6 miles, 89 direct, 71 tornadoes.
