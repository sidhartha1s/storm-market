In June 2025, a tornado tore through Enderlin, North Dakota, derailing a loaded freight train; three people did not survive. The National Weather Service first rated it EF3 — then, four months later, revisited the damage and made it official: EF5, the rarest and most violent rating there is. This is the story of how that number gets decided, and why it took a second look to get it right.
