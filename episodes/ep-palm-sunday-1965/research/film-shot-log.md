Times are min:s on the file's own clock, one decimal. Every start and end was confirmed by reading a frame 0.3 s inside each cut point (montages in `.qc-tmp/films/pairs_*.jpg`); where a frame showed a dissolve or the wrong shot, the time below is the corrected one.

Left out on purpose: every shot with a fallout shelter sign, dose-rate chart or map, nuclear-effects chart or headline, mushroom cloud, or injury. In A14 the brick wall with the fallout shelter sign (0:18.9 to 0:33.3) and the dose-rate map and chart shots are excluded. Many A14 EOC shots between 2:00 and 5:30 show desk signs "RADEF" and "BLAST & FIRE" and are not logged for that reason.

Place, date or agency marks that would misplace the story are in the last column. None of the logged A14 or A15 shots carries a place name.

## Things in these films that would mislead

- A14: the film is about radiological fallout. Desk signs "RADEF" and "BLAST & FIRE" appear in most EOC shots from 2:00 to 5:30; none of those are logged. The end card names the Office of Civil Defense Staff College in Battle Creek, Michigan (13:52 and after), and a map at 10:31 names Cobb AFB. Neither is in a logged shot.
- A13: set in Alaska in 1964 (earthquake) plus flood scenes; "WELFARE ALASKA" on a sleeve at 8:40 and the handwritten map at 3:35 name the place. The 5:49 tornado is of unknown place and date.
- A15: cartoons with a strong teal colour cast; fallout headlines and a "STAY STRONG, the facts about NUCLEAR WEAPONS" title run through it; the closing card at 14:10 is the Prelinger Archives / Filecoin hosting credit and must not be used.
