# 01 · T1-S1 · the toll, 271 (comp `g-t1s1-toll`)

Brief on `references/mg-brief-template.md`. Shared look: `00-design-sheet.md`. Hook tail, first graphic after Paul. Written 12 Oct 26.
**Spoken (T1-S1):** "Two hundred seventy one people lost their lives that day."
**One look:** real wreckage, in three named places, and one number: 271.

## 0. What this graphic claims
271 people lost their lives on April 11, 1965, by the count of four National Weather Service offices; other counts run from 260 to 266. The wreckage is real and each picture is the place its label names.

## 4. Must be true in the picture
1. The figure is `271`. No other figure in the frame is larger than 22 px; 260 and 266 appear only inside the source line.
2. The source line names the National Weather Service and says other counts exist. It is on screen before the comp ends and for at least 1.5 s.
3. Each photograph is under its own label and no other; the label changes on the same frame as the cut.
4. The word under the number is "lives lost". No "killed", "dead" or "deaths" (`references/flag-words.md`).
5. Never black: frame 0 is P1 at full brightness, straight off Paul's last word.
6. No siren icon, no funnel drawing, no tornado photograph (C1 and C6 are not used here).
7. Design sheet S3, S6, S7.

## 5. Text (exact) and cues

The line is about ten words. Three cuts inside it would give each photograph under a second, so P3 lands on the swell after the line. The orchestrator declares a hold of about 2 s after "day" (`<section class="clip hold">`, DESIGN.md) so the source line gets its 1.5 s. The number's slow float (`y` plus or minus 4 px, sine, finite repeats) and the photograph push carry the hold.
