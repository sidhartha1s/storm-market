## 0. What this graphic claims
Media staff told the 1966 researchers that tornado warnings were generally 15 to 30 minutes old when they had them: too old to be truly worthwhile as a warning. It is a comment the study reports as typical of media personnel; it is not a measured figure and it names no station.
