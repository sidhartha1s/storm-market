## 0. What this graphic claims
The city police chief of County B told the researchers why his department did not pass the warning to the public: "I don't like to panic them." The study's own summary is that he was afraid the people might panic unnecessarily if warned directly by the police. Both are the page's words. No city, chief or county is named beyond the study's code.
