## Title (owner, locked)
Who Stopped the Palm Sunday 1965 Tornado Warning?

## Description
https://www.paulraymond.online/yt/?src=desc

The book mentioned at 10:36: The Storm-Ready Home, https://www.paulraymond.online/yt/?src=desc

On Palm Sunday, April 11, 1965, tornadoes crossed six states and 271 people lost their lives. Forecasters in Kansas City had a tornado forecast out by 11:45 that morning, Eastern time. At 5:43 PM the Weather Bureau in South Bend, Indiana, issued a tornado warning and telephoned its warnings down a list: State Police, sheriffs, city police, named radio and television stations. The warnings went to offices and stations, not to homes. This video follows that one message from office to office through the evening, using the Weather Bureau's own logs and a 1966 study of the warning system in Indiana, to find where it stopped and whose job it was to tell the family at the end of the list.
