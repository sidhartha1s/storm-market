## Description

📘 The book mentioned at MM:SS — The Storm-Ready Home, Paul Raymond.

NOAA has declared an El Niño advisory and gives this event a 69% chance of becoming the strongest ever measured since 1950, and its own Oct–Dec outlook puts Texas in the above-normal rain band. This video is about the two dangers Texans do not plan for in December: the winter tornado season (Dec 26, 2015: 12 tornadoes, 13 dead, the first EF4 ever recorded in Texas in December) and flash floods at low-water crossings (more than half of flood deaths happen in vehicles). What the 1997–98 super El Niño did to Southeast Texas (Houston Hobby 79.97 inches, the January 6 1998 flood, Lake Houston three feet over its spillway, 300+ warnings in one winter), what is different now, who gets which winter region by region, what is NOT happening, and the four things to do before December.
