## Description
https://paulraymond.online
📘 The book mentioned at 04:05 — The Storm-Ready Home, Paul Raymond.

NOAA has declared an El Niño advisory and gives this event a 69% chance of becoming the strongest ever measured since 1950. California is first in line. This is what NOAA actually put on the record, why the storm track moves onto the California coast, what February 1998 did to the state (14.89 inches in San Francisco in one month, 17 dead, 2,000 homes), why the 2015–16 "Godzilla" El Niño never delivered for Southern California, what is different this time, how atmospheric-river families work, what is NOT happening, and the four things to do before December.

Sources: NOAA news release (El Niño forms), NOAA Climate Prediction Center ENSO diagnostic discussion and seasonal outlooks, SF Chronicle 1998 retrospective, USGS Fact Sheet 175-99 and landslide maps MF-2325, California Coastal Commission El Niño page, Scripps Institution of Oceanography (2015–16), Journal of Climate 2018, USGS Climate Adaptation Science Centers (atmospheric-river families), Weather West.
