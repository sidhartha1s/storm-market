### A4-S4 — narration
**Picture:** NOAA Report, June 1999, photo of the Millhouse hallway (Joseph Schaefer, NOAA; US gov work), slow push on the standing walls. Chip "30 MIN WARNING · ALL SAFE". Source line: "NOAA Report, June 1999, p2 to p3". [HUMAN] [RS] [VE]
> Now a house. Captain John Millhouse worked at the office that keeps the NEXRAD radars running, and he had about thirty minutes of warning. He took his wife, their two kids, six and two years old, plus the cat and the dog, into the center hallway of their Moore home. When it passed, the walls around that hallway were about all that was left. Every one of them was fine.
