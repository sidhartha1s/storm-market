# ep-moore-1999: angle

## Angle (locked by the owner, 21 Oct 26: ticked "Make it" on the devq topic card)
Claim: One of the best-warned tornadoes on record still took 36 lives, so on May 3, 1999 it came down to where people sheltered, not to the warning.
Spine / arcs:
- Spine: the warning worked; where people sheltered decided who lived.
- Pivot: the first "Tornado Emergency", issued at 6:57 p.m., 25 minutes before the tornado entered Moore and south Oklahoma City.
- Why: 10 of the 36 direct deaths were in mobile homes, all at Bridge Creek in Grady County; in the same county, 35 people from a mostly mobile-home neighbourhood crammed into one storm cellar and all survived.
- Small arc: one TV station urged people to get out of the tornado's path; framed as "do not copy this". The station stays unnamed, as in the source.
Source: NWS Service Assessment "Oklahoma/Southern Kansas Tornado Outbreak of May 3, 1999" (`research/nws-sa-ok-ks99.pdf`): lead times p9 (document page numbers), Tornado Emergency p10 and p15, TV station p11, cellar p22, overpasses p23 (Finding 10). Death list: Brooks, NSSL "Oklahoma tornado deaths, 3 May 1999" (`research/brooks-okcdeath.txt`, Wayback copy of 27 Jun 2021).
Ruled out as the lead: the 301 mph Doppler on Wheels wind measurement (the brief said 318 mph; to be checked) and the overpass story.
Still unverified: "best-warned on record" as a superlative (needs a source or softer wording); the minute Bridge Creek was hit (for "more than an hour of warning" at Bridge Creek itself).

## What the fact check moved (21 Oct 26, orchestrator, against the two sources above)
- **Hook number corrected.** The draft hook said "Oklahoma City had sixty-five minutes of warning". The 65 minutes is Grady County's lead time (SA p9: Grady 65, McClain 18, Cleveland 31, Oklahoma County 13 minutes). Oklahoma City had 13. The hook now places the 65 minutes in Grady County, which is also where the 10 mobile-home deaths and the 35-person cellar were. Wording change, same meaning.
- **36 holds**, with its scope: Brooks lists 36 direct deaths for this tornado (3 apartment, 2 business building, 10 mobile home, 2 under overpasses, 19 in houses) plus 5 indirect. The Service Assessment says 38 (p1); the script uses 36 and says "direct" nowhere it would confuse.
- **Mobile homes: 10**, not 11. All 10 are at Bridge Creek (Brooks M1 to M10). An 11th mobile-home death (Shawnee) was a different tornado.
- **Tornado Emergency 6:57 p.m.** confirmed (SA p10, p15, Appendix C "1857 SVS"); "25 minutes before the tornado entered those areas" is the SA's own wording (p15).
- **35 in one cellar** confirmed (SA p22, Grady County, "an area with primarily mobile homes").
- **TV station** confirmed and unnamed (SA p11): its statement "was cited as one of the reasons many people fled the path of the tornado"; the SA adds fleeing "is not recommended but was effective in this particular event". The script must carry both halves.
- **New fact that sharpens the spine**: 19 of the 36 were inside houses, 2 of them under a staircase at Bridge Creek. A house above ground was not a shelter from this tornado; a hole in the ground was.
