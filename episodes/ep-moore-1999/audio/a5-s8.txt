Then came May thirty-first, eleven days later. Slow storms crept toward Oklahoma City on a Friday, right around rush hour, when a lot of people were already in their cars. Some weather professionals told listeners they would not survive unless they were underground. Driving south became one of the main pieces of advice that day. It's a cousin of the advice one station gave in 1999, the one I asked you not to copy. This time, large numbers of people took it, and the roads jammed. At one that afternoon, the weather office in Norman had posted a caution about exactly that.
