📘 The book mentioned at 7:42

On May 3, 1999, the tornado that crossed Bridge Creek and Moore, Oklahoma, came with more warning than almost any tornado before it: Grady County had sixty five minutes, and at 6:57 p.m. the Weather Service issued its first ever Tornado Emergency, twenty five minutes before the funnel entered Moore and south Oklahoma City. Thirty six people still died. Ten of them were in mobile homes at Bridge Creek, nineteen were inside houses, and in the same county thirty five people from a mobile home neighbourhood packed into one storm cellar and every one of them lived. This is what the warning did that evening, and why where people sheltered decided who came home.
