### Does it stay inside the sources?

## One judgment on the whole script's ending
The ending is honest and it is useful, and it is not the film's peak. The answer arrives early in Act 5 (A5-S2), is
supported for two minutes, and is then said three times running: the list in A5-S12, Paul in A5-S13, and the map and
table again in Z1. A viewer who has watched for twenty minutes gets confirmation where a turn should be. Two changes
fix most of it without new material: give the title line to Paul alone, and put the film's most surprising usable
fact, that people came through the Moore E F five above ground in places they had picked beforehand, as late as
possible, directly ahead of the answer. The one thing that must change before anyone hears it is A5-S5.
