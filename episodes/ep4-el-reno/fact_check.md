## What this changes outside the intro
The earlier research note and the owner page described "the one that hit the TV crew" and "the one that reached the research car" as if they were two vortices. The paper points to one: racing across US-81 at about 56 mph when it met the S U V, nearly stopped over Reuter Road when it met the car about four minutes later. Acts 2 and 3 are written to that.
