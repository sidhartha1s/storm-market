# ep4-el-reno · family "cards" · brief (10 Oct 26)

Twelve mounted comps (mounts.json family "cards") plus the closing channel card. Template: `references/mg-brief-template.md`.
Cues are comp-local seconds = `audio/<segment>.words.json` word starts (each host starts with its segment's voice).

## 0. What the family claims
Names, attributed sentences and three small schematics (a clock rail, a timeline bar, a marker list). No comp states a
number, place or quote that is not in `fact_check.md` or the segment's Picture note. Nothing is drawn as fake footage.

## 1. Sources
- NWS Service Assessment (SA): "unable to survive if not underground", "sheltering in place", the Rita comparison, "some of
  the most experienced and safety-minded chasers", worst Oklahoma City flood for lives lost, most flash flood lives lost
  in the Norman area since 1934, Appendix C ages (55, 24, 45, 65; a 35-year-old with no name).
- Weather, Climate, and Society 14(3), 2022 (`research/facts.md`, not re-pulled): six metro areas, 2011 to 2018.
- weather.com (`research/facts.md`, not re-pulled): the Bettes and Anderson quotes.
- Local TV, 2014 (`research/facts.md`, not re-pulled): Henderson's 6:05 PM photograph, about ten minutes, sons Robbie and Wyatt.
- OUN event page: Moore EF5 on May 20, 2013; US-81 crossed at 5:19 PM CST (6:19 PM CDT). W14: sub-vortex over Reuter Road
  2323:35 to 2323:57 UTC (not printed as a clock time, see below).
- Marshall et al., damage survey: the EF3 sentence.
- Photo in a comp: `media/pd/nssl-doppler-on-wheels-dow7-1.jpg` (NOAA NSSL, 30 May 2015, not 2013), captioned on screen
  with what it is. "University of Oklahoma" is text only: a photo of a different radar under that name would mislead
  (orchestrator, 10 Oct 26).
- I1-S7 second clock mark carries no clock time: `fact_check.md` lines 28 and 60 give the sub-vortex over Reuter Road
  from 2323:35 to 2323:57 UTC (it rounds to 6:24 PM and is not stated as the strike time), and the roads family prints
  6:22:15 PM on Reuter Road (headlights, line 60). The US-81 mark "6:19 PM" is line 28 (OUN, 5:19 PM CST).

## 4. MUST BE TRUE IN THE PICTURE
1. Every quote is the exact string in section 5 with its source line; no quote card for A1-S6 or A4-S12.
2. Death words: only "lost"; never "killed", "died", "deadliest".
3. Names are text only. A3-S10 and I1-S7: "Tim Samaras · 55", "Paul Samaras · 24", "Carl Young · 45". A4-S12: only
   "Mike Morgan · KFOR" and "Gary England · KWTV, a competing station".
4. Henderson's age is not shown (fact_check: his age stays tagged; the table row has no name).
5. A1-S6: the 6:03 touchdown mark sits two minutes left of 6:05; the head travels ten minutes (660 px) and stops at "cut out". The stop is labelled "about", never a clock time.
6. A4-S8: twelve pins in two groups (7 and 5), a thirteenth apart labelled "65"; the bar has two end labels and nothing between.
7. A5-S3: four rows (house, barn, tree, power pole), each with a box. No box is ever ticked; the house and barn boxes are the ones marked empty under "Where the sub-vortices went".
8. A1-S5: eight pins, four lit ("half"), no map; no place is claimed for the pin beyond "Near El Reno".
9. A2-S4: the one photograph (DOW 7) is not presented as 31 May 2013; its caption says what it is. No photograph sits under "University of Oklahoma".
10. Nothing lands before its spoken word (cue table). A3-S10: names only on the last two sentences; no motion in the last second.
11. Overlay comps keep the root transparent; text sits on a glass card (or the red popfig with its local scrim).
