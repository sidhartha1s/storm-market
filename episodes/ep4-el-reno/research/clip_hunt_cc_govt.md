## Lane 1 — YouTube Creative Commons search (largely a dead lane)

Ran `yt-dlp --match-filter "license*=Creative Commons"` across four search terms (40-50
results each: "oklahoma tornado 2013", "moore oklahoma tornado", "el reno oklahoma tornado",
"oklahoma city flood 2013") — **zero results matched the CC-BY license filter.** A broader
unfiltered scan of ~140 videos across the same terms also came back with the license field
either `None` or `NA` for every result checked, including plausible non-chaser civilian uploads
(Richard Miller, Jeffrey Lechus, Val and Amy Castor, nealgentry, grant rohlmeier, The Oklahoman
Video Archive, centralokstorms, Auce Java) — none carry a YouTube CC-BY license.

69. **Lane verdict:** YouTube's own CC-BY filter is functionally empty for this event. The two
    CC-licensed video assets that do exist for El Reno (items #46 and #47 above) live on
    Wikimedia Commons, sourced from YouTube uploads that were individually CC-BY-licensed by
    their storm-chaser uploaders — and both trip the channel's chaser-footage ban regardless of
    the license tag. **Net finding: this event has no clean CC-YouTube lane.** The 40+ raw
    search results (Reed Timmer, Dan Robinson, Pecos Hank, TheF5hunter, StormChasingVideo,
    Storm Chaser Daniel Shaw, Tom Pastrano, etc.) are entirely storm-chaser or chaser-adjacent
    content, all-rights-reserved by default, all correctly excluded per house rule.
