## Footage / Visual Style
- **Dominant asset: animated satellite/Google-Maps-style overlay** — red circle "storm cone"
  tracking a moving red dot, with colored crosshair pins labeled with each chaser's name
  (Richard Henderson, Wright & Sullivan, Reed Timmer, Twistex, Dan Robinson) pinned to their

- **Radar imagery**: 2-3 frames of real NEXRAD reflectivity (green/yellow/red bands over a road

- **Found footage inserts (short, few seconds each):** dashcam/POV shots looking out a car
  windshield at a dark grey funnel (2 uses), a brief "BREAKING NOW — TORNADO NEAR OKLAHOMA CITY"
  news chyron insert (looks like a TWC/local-news broadcast pull), a still photo of a wrecked
  pickup truck with "TORNADO 2013" grease-pen text on the door.
- **Graphic callout:** a "2.6 MILES" width-measurement graphic drawn directly on the map —
  cheap, single-frame, high information density.
- **Grading:** heavy blue/teal desaturated grade over the map graphics; found-footage clips are
  naturally dark/grey (storm light), not color-matched to the map segments — visible grade
  mismatch between the two asset types.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts throughout both contact sheets — no fade-to-black or crossfades
  detected anywhere in the 25-frame or 20-frame sampling. Continuity is carried by the map's
  own pan/zoom motion (Ken-Burns-style camera move on the graphic layer), not by transition FX.
- **Text/graphic density:** high relative to duration — nearly every frame carries a persistent
  label (chaser name), plus recurring radar/map chrome. Feels cluttered but information-dense;
  works because the audio is doing zero explaining, so the graphic layer has to carry the whole
  story.

## Steal / Avoid
**Steal:**
- The **animated map + colored chaser-position pins** as a lightweight, license-free way to show
  multiple simultaneous story threads (Henderson / Wright & Sullivan / Timmer / TWISTEX / Bettes /
  Robinson) converging in real time — cheap, clear, zero footage-license risk, and directly
  answers viewer confusion seen in comments about "who was where."
- A single bold on-map measurement callout ("2.6 MILES") as a graphic beat — matches our
  "each stat translated to human scale" rule.
- Raw NWS warning audio as a layered SFX/VO bed under our own narration for authenticity (already
  PD-safe per our footage rules).
**Avoid:**
- No context/superlative/date on screen — we need this baked in from frame one (title/lower-third).
- Mismatched grade between map graphics and found footage — unify grading per our broadcast
  rule.
- No narrative arc or emotional close — leaving viewers to reconstruct the human story themselves
  in comments is a gap, not a feature; our format banks the emotion at the end deliberately.
- Reliance on unlicensed-looking chaser dashcam clips — per our footage rules, any TWISTEX/
  Henderson/Sullivan/Robinson/Timmer chaser footage needs a license before use; this reference
  video's clips are NOT cleared assets for us regardless of how well they cut.
