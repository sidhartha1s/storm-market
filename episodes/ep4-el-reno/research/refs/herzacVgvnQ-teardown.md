## Steal / avoid list
**Steal:**
- Cold-open on real found audio/dialogue with zero narration for the first ~4s.
- Consistent color grade unifying mixed-source footage into one visual identity.
- Dramatic-irony technique: show a real, mundane chaser quote, then narrator recontextualizes it
  as tragic in hindsight.
- Minimal on-screen text/graphics — let real broadcast lower-thirds and radar imagery carry
  visual density instead of adding narrator graphic clutter.
- Hard-cut editing rhythm (no fades) keeps energy up during the chase sequence.

**Avoid:**
- Never source footage/audio without license or credit — this exact video is the cautionary
  tale, actively being called out in its own comments.
- Don't oversell a superlative title claim the audience can fact-check and reject (EF3 vs EF5,
  "worst" vs "largest").
- Don't bank the emotional climax in the middle and close on generic reflection — hold the
  human/emotional payoff for the true end per our own structure rule.
- Don't skip the meteorology-explainer depth — this video's shallow science layer is a stated
  content gap in its own comments; our explainer-every-episode rule directly fills it.
