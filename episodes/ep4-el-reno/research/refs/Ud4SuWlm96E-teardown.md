## Steal / avoid list

**Steal:**
- Cold-open on 45-60s of pure raw chaser audio/footage with NO narrator VO — let the found-footage chaos do the hooking work before narration starts.
- "11 days earlier" flashback-hook device to set stakes/scale before returning to main timeline.
- Named-quote lower-third cards (expert name + verbatim quote) for authority beats.
- Individual survivor vignette (named person) layered into the mass-casualty-averted narrative for a human-scale anchor.
- Mid-video "hero" character introduction (Tim Samaras/TWISTEX) rather than front-loading every character in the cold open.

**Avoid:**
- Emotional peak (TWISTEX deaths) landing at ~55-60% mark instead of the close — we should keep our locked "emotion banked at the end" discipline, since audience response here (per comments) proves the emotional beat is the most memorable part; ending on it (not a rating-controversy coda) would likely land harder.
- Using audio that provokes an authenticity controversy in comments (real vs "representation only") without on-screen disclosure — label recreated/dramatized audio explicitly.
- Generic auto-thumbnail instead of a designed cinematic-AI thumbnail (our formula already mandates better than this).
- Leaving a documented content gap (Richard Henderson, rating-controversy resolution) unaddressed when it's an easy, sourceable differentiation win.
