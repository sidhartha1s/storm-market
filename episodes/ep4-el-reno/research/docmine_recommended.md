# Recommended segments — NWS Norman "Lessons" documentary

Full shot-by-shot inventory: `research/docmine_inventory.md`. This file lists only segments clean of both hard exclusions (private photo) and credit-flags (named chaser/videographer footage), each mapped to a specific `edit_script.md` narration beat.

**Honesty note on count:** the brief asked for 25–40 recommended segments. This documentary turned out to be a crowd-sourced reconstruction where nearly every raw ground-level chase clip is individually credited to a named videographer (confirmed by the film's own end-credit card — see inventory). That excludes most of the visually strongest tornado-on-the-ground shots from safe use. What's left clean is almost entirely **NWS/NOAA-native analytical graphics** — satellite/radar loops, maps, meteorology diagrams — not raw footage of the tornado itself. 27 segments below are genuinely clean (no personal name anywhere in shot). A further 9 "MEDIUM-RISK" radar/map graphics carry a named position-pin (the underlying map is NWS's own product, but it identifies a specific chaser's tracked location) — listed separately at the end for an owner call, not folded into the clean count.

All timecodes are `source_in–source_out` (seconds into the documentary) and `our_ep_timestamp` (estimated placement in our episode, proportional to the act's word budget in `edit_script.md` — will shift slightly once TTS timing locks).
