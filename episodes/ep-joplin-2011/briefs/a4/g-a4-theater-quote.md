## 0. What this graphic claims
56 people were in the Stained Glass Theater when the tornado hit. Around the first siren the director told them,
verbatim: "We have a strong building here and anyone who wants to stay is welcome to do so. We can all go down in the
basement." About 25 minutes later she told those still there: "we want you to get to the basement right now".
