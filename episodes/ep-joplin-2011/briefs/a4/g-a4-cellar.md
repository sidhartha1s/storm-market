## 0. What this graphic claims
The Macadoodles had a concrete wine cellar about 20 feet down. The manager, with other staff, led six customers (one a
baby) down into it, twelve people in all. The wood-frame building above was demolished, and no one was hurt: twelve
people below ground, all OK. The zero below ground in this film is about the people the tornado took, never about everyone.

## 1. SOURCE
- B35 "The wine cellar was a big, 1,000 ft2 basement-like structure, encased in concrete, approximately 20 ft deep" NIST Sec. 4.4.3 (end).
- B34 "Six customers (including a baby) and six managers and employees were in the store" Sec. 4.4.3. 6 + 6 = 12 (script).
- B49 "the manager, with help from other employees, led the six customers downstairs and into the wine cellar." Sec. 4.4.3.
- B33 "No injuries or fatalities occurred in the Macadoodles that day, even though the wood-frame building, with wood roof trusses, was demolished by the storm." Sec. 4.4.3.
- facts.md NOTE under B49: "The 0 is 'no evidence any of those killed were below ground', not 'no one was below ground'."
