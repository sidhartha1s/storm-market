## Description

Joplin, Missouri, May 22, 2011. A hundred sixty-one people lost their lives, and the town's tornado sirens went off that evening. Most of the people the tornado took were doing what you would do: they got inside. This is what the federal investigators found when they walked the damage path building by building, and what the sirens actually sounded like to the people who heard them.
