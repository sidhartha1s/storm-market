## The lines the claim rests on (read again in the source text, 15 Oct 26)
- NIST Finding 40: "Of the 155 impact-related fatalities, 135 (87 percent) involved persons who are known to have been located inside structures during the tornado." Executive Summary: "83.8 percent, or 135 of the 161 deaths".
- NIST Finding 46: "No fatalities occurred in demolished, detached homes in which people took refuge in basements. Additionally, NIST found no evidence that any of those killed were located underground during the tornado."
- NIST Finding 20: "There were no community shelters or safe rooms in the City of Joplin or Jasper County ... Also, 82 percent of the homes in Joplin lacked basements."
- NIST Finding 31: "Despite public perception, no evidence was found of high false-alarm rates for Joplin's outdoor siren system. Since 2007, the average rate of activation of the 25-siren outdoor warning system in Joplin was once per year (at most), not including the test activations (1 minute in duration) that occurred weekly."
- NIST Finding 30: "From 2005 through 2011, 78 percent (14 out of 18) of the official tornado warnings issued for Joplin did not result in a verified tornado; this percentage was in line with the 2007-2011 national average storm-based tornado false-alarm rate of 74.7 percent."
- NIST Finding 47: "approximately 8 fatalities occurred per thousand people in Joplin aged 60 years and over compared with 2 fatalities per thousand people in Joplin under 60 years."
- NWS timeline: "511 pm CDT - Initial 3 minute siren alert sounded for Jasper County and Joplin"; "534 pm CDT - Approximate initial Tornado touchdown"; "538 pm CDT - Second 3 minute siren alert sounded for Jasper County and Joplin."
- NWS Finding 2c and summary: "the perceived frequency of siren activation in Joplin led the majority of survey participants to become desensitized or complacent to this method of warning." Residents told them they "hear sirens all the time and [sirens] go off for dark clouds".

## Limits the facts put on the script
- "Below ground" stays "not one person the investigators could place". NIST says "no evidence", never "nobody".
- "Once a year" means activations outside the weekly test. Joplin tested its sirens every week for 1 minute (NIST Finding 31); June First tells this as 52 tests a year. The script must say both, or a viewer who heard the Monday test is right to object. NIST also says the sirens sounded about once a year for non-tornadic high-wind events.
- "The sirens did not lie" is the record's side, not a verdict on the warning system. The NWS assessment found the first siren lost credibility; the script never says "the sirens were not the problem".
- The two numbers are different sets: 14 of 18 is tornado warnings 2005 to 2011, "once a year" is siren activations since 2007. Never merge them into one figure.
- Siren count: NIST says a 25-siren system; June First says 24; our old script said 28. Use NIST.
- Deaths: 155 impact-related (NIST's base for the 135), 161 in NIST's executive summary, 158 direct plus 3 indirect in Storm Data. Each number is said with its base.
