📘 The book mentioned at 11:54

On the night of August 19, 1969, the remains of Hurricane Camille stalled over the Blue Ridge Mountains and dropped more rain on Nelson County, Virginia, than the weather records of the day had ever seen. By morning the mountainsides had come down into the valleys, the roads and bridges were gone, and the county was cut off from the rest of the state. This film follows that night, how the hillsides gave way, and the search that followed, when every count of the missing was a different number. It ends with a question that Virginia is still asking today, more than fifty years on.
