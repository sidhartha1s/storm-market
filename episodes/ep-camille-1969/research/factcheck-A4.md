## Findings that change the act (read first)

1. DATE. The VDH archive heading is August 28, 2026, but WSET and CBS19 published on Aug 27, 2026 (before that heading). So "posted on August twenty-eighth" and "three days later it was on local TV" are both wrong as stated. 29News ran Aug 31.
2. CONTACT. The VDH notice itself names Lara Newell and lara.newell@vdh.virginia.gov; the OCME_CENT@vdh.virginia.gov address is the Central District OCME mailbox (VDH-MP: also phone (804) 786-3174) and is the address WSET, WJLA and 29News print for this case. Both are real. OCME_CENT is fine for the card.
3. AGES. Three different age sets exist: 1969 News & Advance list; WHRO/Newell (four children 6 to 19, woman about 70, two women 35 to 40, man about 50); NamUs descriptions in CBS19 (15 to 19, 12 to 14, 70 to 75, 10 to 12, 6 to 8, 40 to 60, about 50, 35 to 40); WSET/29News (seven only). "Seventeen or younger" is true only of the 1969 list.
4. BREMO. Bremo Power Station is at Bremo Bluff, FLUVANNA County (Wiki-Bremo: "unincorporated community located on the northern bank of the James River in Fluvanna County"). CBS19 (NamUs) puts the girl "in the James River, about seven or eight miles upriver from the Bremo Bluff Plant." Not Nelson County. Wingina (near Buford Island) is in Nelson County (Wikipedia). No independent source for "Buford Island" found (web search: none); it rests on the 1969 list alone.
5. DNA. No Camille source says "no DNA test in 1969". WHRO says the opposite framing: cremation makes DNA testing impossible "until technological advances potentially make the process available on cremains."
6. FAMILY ON VACATION. WHRO: "potentially a family or families on vacation, Newell said, traveling through Virginia." The 1969 list adds: adults "believed to be traveling on U.S. 29"; children "believed to have been traveling together". So the eight may not be Nelson locals. A4-S14's "kid in Nelson County" framing conflicts with this.
