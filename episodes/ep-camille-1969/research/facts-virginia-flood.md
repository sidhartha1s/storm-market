## Conflicts

- C1. Peak rain and period. S1: "27-28 inches" in about 8 hours (central Nelson), with "a near record 28-inch total catch" elsewhere in the same report; S2: "about 28 inches" in 8 hours; S5: maximum 27 in, "3-5 hour period"; S6 (NWS Sterling): "31 inches of rain in six hours"; widely published "31 inches near Tye River" was explicitly not verified by the Weather Bureau survey (V6). Resolution: 27-28 in in about 8 hours is the primary-source figure; the 27 in reading is a trash-barrel catch rated Good (V5). 31 is unverified and 46 is unconfirmed. Hours: 8 (S1, S2) beats 6 (S6) and 3-5 (S5, a 2016 compilation); intensity was highest in a shorter burst but no primary source gives a measured peak hour.
- C2. Start time of rain. S1 says the 8-hour period began about 7 p.m.; S2 says about 2030; a Nelson gauge remark says 8 p.m. to 4 a.m. Pick "about 8 p.m. in Nelson" (S2 + gauge remark) or hedge as "evening".
- C3. Death counts. S1 (Nov 13, 1969): 113 dead + 39 missing; S2: about 150, 125 in Nelson County; S7 (secondary): 153 dead or missing in Virginia, 126 in Nelson County; S6: "at least 153 Virginians", 126 in Nelson County; S8: 124 in Virginia (and also quotes 113 + 39 in its timeline). Not resolved here; the toll slice owns it. "About 124 killed in Nelson County" in the angle draft is not matched by any primary source read: S2 says 125 in Nelson.
- C4. Davis Creek toll. S1 (Nov 1969): 27 known dead and 22 missing in the Davis Creek hollow (49 total). S7 (secondary): 54 killed along a 4-mile stretch of Davis Creek and Huffman's Hollow. Different areas and counts; not reconciled.
- C5. Damage. S1 p. 39: $116,460,550 total in the James River basin (state estimate). S2: "Damage in Nelson County alone amounted to $116 million". Not reconciled; do not say "Nelson alone" without S2's wording and a caveat.
- C6. Forecast narrative. S8 says the storm "made a sharp turn to the east" to the forecasters' surprise; S1 gives a track east "south of Roanoke and Lynchburg" and rapid intensification over the ridges. S6/S8 describe no warning; S1 says Richmond got "advance warning" of the river rise. Both can be true (no mountain warning, but river forecasts for the James), but the timing of any warning is not documented in the texts read.
- C7. Schwarz vs USGS on Blue Ridge lift. S3 (abstract via search summary, UNVERIFIED) mentions "some orographic enhancement"; S2 says the effect of orography "is difficult to determine" and points to 21 inches near a non-mountainous area.
