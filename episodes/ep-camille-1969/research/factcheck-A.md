## 2. I1-S8 "more than a hundred twenty" and "about one in every hundred": CONFIRMED

- NWS Sterling, https://weather.gov/lwx/hurricane_history (RAW): "At least 153 Virginians lost their lives in Camille; of these, 126 were residents of Nelson County."
- NWS Sterling flood-states-va, https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood-states-va (RAW): "In Nelson County alone, the death toll was 123." and "Overall, 153 deaths were attributed to the flooding from Camille".
- All Nelson counts on disk are 123 to 126 (plus 124, 125). "More than a hundred twenty" holds for every one. Do not use the marker's 114 + 37.
- USGS OFR 99-518 (URL that works: https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/1999/ofr-99-0518/ofr-99-0518.html; the old ofr-99-518 path 404s). RAW, Mitigation section: "The storm in August 1969 killed 150 people, including about 1 percent of the citizens of Nelson County, a rural and sparsely settled area."
- Davis Creek warning, same section, full sentences: "If both evacuation plans and a warning system had been in place in Nelson County during 1969, the citizens along Davis Creek would have been advised just before midnight of August 19 to seek refuge at a nearby structure not in the path of streams draining steep slopes, such as the Oak Hill Church. Had all the citizens heard and heeded this warning, most or all of the 50 fatalities in that one area could have been avoided."
- 1970 census, primary, US Census Bureau, 1970 Census of Population PC(1)-A48 Virginia, Table 9 "Population and Land Area of Counties: 1970 and 1960", printed page 48-16: Nelson, land area 471 sq mi, 1970 population 11,702 (24.8 per sq mi), 1960 population 12,752, change -8.2 percent. https://www2.census.gov/library/publications/decennial/1970/population-volume-1/00496492v1p48ch02.pdf (PDF page 16). Read: RAW, scan with no text layer, read by eye from a rendered page. This replaces the Wikipedia-only P24.
- Math: 124 / 11,702 = 1.06 percent; 126 / 11,702 = 1.08 percent (about 1 in 94 to 1 in 93). "About one in every hundred" is within rounding and matches the USGS wording.

## 3. VDH appeal

- Primary: VDH media-update archive, https://www.vdh.virginia.gov/news/media-update/previous-media-updates/ (RAW), item "OCME Missing Persons Program", listed under the heading "August 28, 2026" (news copies run 31 Aug 2026). No standalone release found. Raw: "The Office of the Chief Medical Examiner (OCME) is working to raise awareness and generate new leads that may help identify eight unidentified individuals whose remains were discovered following the devastation caused by Hurricane Camille in August 1969 in Nelson County, Virginia." and "In total, 124 people died in Nelson County and eight fatalities are still unidentified."
- The primary gives no ages and no river. Only "Nelson County".
- WHRO / Radio IQ, "57 years later, Virginia tries to identify eight Hurricane Camille victims", published 13 Oct 2026 4:25 AM EDT, https://www.whro.org/2026-10-13/57-years-later-virginia-tries-to-identify-eight-hurricane-camille-victims (RAW; local DNS fails for whro.org, fetched with curl --resolve). Raw: "attempting to identify the remains of eight people from the storm." / "seven of the bodies were found in Nelson, while another was found nearby, over the Albemarle County line." / "four children possibly between 6 and 19, a woman about 70, two women around 35 to 40, and a 50-year old man". WHRO is the only copy that lists eight by age (4+1+2+1 = 8).
- 29News/WVIR, 31 Aug 2026, https://www.29news.com/2026/08/31/virginia-department-health-seeks-help-identifying-8-hurricane-camille-victims/ (RAW): "recovered around the Rockfish River in Nelson County" and "two adults, one older adult, two teens, and two children" (seven). WSET/WJLA (RAW): "Woods Mill area around the Rockfish River"; "two adults aged 30 to 50 years, an older woman aged 50 to 70 years, two teenagers aged 15 to 19 and two children around the ages of 8 to 12" (seven). Both news copies list only seven; the primary does not list ages at all.
- 1969 list (N&A, RAW), the eight unidentified: female about 70 (Buford Island, James River above the Rockfish mouth); female 35 to 40 (Woods Mill); female late 40s to early 50s (one mile below Woods Mill); male late 50s (Albemarle shore at Howardsville); male about 17 (below Schuyler); male 12 to 14; female about 10; female 6 to 8 (above Bremo Power Plant, James River). Six listed "From Rockfish River", two from the James.
- T1-S2 verdict: CORRECTED. "Recovered along the Rockfish River" overstates. Replacement: "...but those eight were found in Nelson County, in the river country around the Rockfish River." Ages: do not put seven ages on screen. Safe if needed: "four of them children" (WHRO "four children possibly between 6 and 19"; 1969 list has four aged 17 or under).
- Date and count for T1-S3: "fifty-seven years" holds (1969 to 2026). "Eight" CONFIRMED by VDH primary and WHRO.
