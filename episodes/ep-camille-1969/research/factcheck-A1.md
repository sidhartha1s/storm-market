## Marker 1. A1-S2 forecast card (P27): CORRECTED
Draft: card "a chance of showers, clearing in the morning" attributed "the local forecast, as quoted by C-VILLE Weekly"; spoken "The forecast people remember called for...".
Raw, C-VILLE, Lisa Provence, 14 Aug 2019 (published_time 2019-08-14), https://c-ville.com/?p=141343 , RAW: "The local forecast called for a chance of showers, clearing in the morning. 'The fact it roared back to life in Virginia was a tremendous surprise to everyone,' says Halverson." The first sentence is the reporter's own voice, not a quote and not a recollection by residents. Better, RAW: Radio IQ (WVTF), Sandy Hausman, "Why The Disaster of the Century Hit Nelson County", 23 Aug 2019, https://www.wvtf.org/news/2019-08-23/why-the-disaster-of-the-century-hit-nelson-county : "When residents of Nelson County tuned into the evening news on August 19, 1969, they heard an unexceptional weather forecast according to Jeff Halverson, a professor of meteorology at the University of Maryland - Baltimore County. 'There was no forecast for any type of flood,' he recalls. 'The weather bureau at the time simply stated there will be showers tonight, clearing in the morning.'" Halverson recalls it; no 1969 forecast text exists in our research (none found; factcheck-B: no advisories after 18 Aug in the Corps table).
Replacement card: "the weather bureau at the time simply stated there will be showers tonight, clearing in the morning" · Jeff Halverson, meteorology professor, UMBC · Radio IQ (WVTF), 23 Aug 2019.
