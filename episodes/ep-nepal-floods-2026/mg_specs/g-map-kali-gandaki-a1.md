# g-map-kali-gandaki-a1 · A1 "gorge narrows" beat (about 26 s)

Library source: `episodes/ep-enderlin-2025/compositions/g-master-map.html` (cam, rail, rows, pin ring), retuned to a zoomed gorge.

## 0. Claim
In a gorge this narrow, debris entering the channel can dam the river: water backs up upstream ("blocked"), and a breach sends a surge
downstream ("surge risk"). On Sept 28 reports said the Kali Gandaki had been blocked for a while before the water cut through.

## 1. Source
- Landslide-dam mechanism: factcheck-A row 12 (CONFIRMED, general geohazard science).
- The Kali Gandaki debris did dam the river, a temporary lake formed, the water cut through within about a day: factcheck-C row 12.
- Collapse near Lete, Mustang: factcheck-C rows 2 and 8. River: OSM ways "Kali Gandaki" (ODbL), full detail at this zoom.
- Peaks and towns: Dhaulagiri 28.6961 N 83.4953 E, Annapurna I 28.5961 N 83.8203 E, Jomsom 28.7833 N 83.7306 E (Wikipedia API), Lete
  28.62 N 83.60 E (factcheck-C row 14).
- No published figure gives the dam's size, the lake's extent or which wall failed. **Debris, dam, lake and arrows are SCHEMATIC** and
  labelled so on screen; the east-wall source is chosen because the clip was filmed from across the river near Lete (factcheck-C row 2).

## 2. Datum, scale, orientation
North up. Same projection as the other Nepal maps. Camera: world (-51, -30) at screen (620, 540), **32 px per mile**
(`translate(2252,1500) scale(32)`). Scale bar 64 px = 2 mi. The camera opens at 0.82 and pushes to 1.00 about the dam point in 1.6 s
("zoom on the gorge"); labels enter after the push. River channel drawn 9 px wide (real width well under 1 px here): exaggerated, noted.

## 3. Geometry (screen px at cam 1.00)
Dam point on the river (538, 550), flow there runs south-east. Lake = the river's own path from the dam 186 px upstream to (533, 426),
28 px wide. Debris arrow (660, 470) -> (556, 540). Upstream "Blocked" arrow (478, 528) -> (430, 452), label end-anchored (420, 500).
Downstream "Surge risk" arrow (628, 612) -> (622, 738), label (650, 705). Lete dot (501.6, 571.4). Dhaulagiri (298, 403),
Annapurna I (930, 624), Jomsom (756, 210). Rail (right 820 px, text at x 1270, all above y 680).

## 4. Must be true
1. "Blocked" points UPSTREAM (north-west, against the flow); "Surge risk" points DOWNSTREAM (south).
2. The lake sits upstream of the dam only, never below it.
3. The debris enters from the bank opposite Lete (east), and the dam sits across the channel.
4. After "cut through again" the dam is visibly breached and the lake draws back.
5. SCHEMATIC note on screen; no size, depth or duration numbers invented.
