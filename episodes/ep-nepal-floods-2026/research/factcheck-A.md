## Other factual issues noticed in H/HT/I/A1, not tagged
1. **Conflicting nationwide death tolls, same two days, none reconciled in the script.** The script picks NewsBytes' 24 dead/14 missing (tag 4), but Al Jazeera/Kathmandu Post (NDRRMA-sourced) had 12 on 27 Sep, an Al Jazeera video had 14 dead/~11-12 missing on 28 Sep, and ANI had an unconfirmed 21 dead/5 missing. The script doesn't flag that this is a moving, contested number — worth a script-level note even though the chosen figure and its attribution are individually correct.
2. **The displacement stat (tag 5, "1,500 / 143 houses") and the death-toll stat (tag 4, "24 / 14") come from two different NewsBytes articles with two different geographic scopes** (one nationwide, one Gandaki-corridor-only) stitched into adjacent sentences with no scope marker. A viewer would reasonably assume both numbers describe the same footprint. Consider a one-word scope cue ("across the valley" vs "nationwide").
3. **Line 162 tag (frame-check, out of my scope) interacts with a real discrepancy I found while searching**: at least one outlet (PressAdda) describes the mountain section going into the river "in just six seconds," not three. This directly affects the frame-check beat another agent owns — flagging so it isn't missed.
4. Minor: the gorge-vs-Grand-Canyon comparison (tag 3) mixes two different depth conventions (peak-to-river for Kali Gandaki vs rim-to-river for Grand Canyon). The numbers are individually correct and the script's own wording already frames it as "river up to the summit," so this isn't a factual error, just worth knowing it's not a true apples-to-apples "canyon depth" comparison if a sharp-eyed viewer checks.
