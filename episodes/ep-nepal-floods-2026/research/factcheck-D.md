## 3. Himlung Himal avalanche search

**Verdict: CONFIRMED, with early-report conflicts noted**

- Total at base camp: 16 (14 kitchen staff from Himalayan Holidays + 2 climbing guides from Imagine Nepal Treks). Avalanche struck early 27 Sept 2026, Manang district.
- By 28 Sept 2026 (Explorersweb, "Himlung Avalanche: Four Bodies Found, No Survivors" — https://explorersweb.com/himlung-avalanche-four-bodies-found-no-survivors/): 4 recovered (Chhebi Bhote, Pasang Bhote found 27 Sept; Birendra Gurung, Ghan Shyam Rai found 28 Sept). 12 still missing. No survivors reported; rescuers quoted saying survival is very unlikely given 15+ feet of snow.
- Search: a helicopter reached the camp the evening of 27 Sept once weather cleared; ground search led by Mingma G (Imagine Nepal) with an 8-member Sherpa team. No army/military involvement found in sources checked for this specific site (distinct from Myagdi, where Kathmandu Post reports two helicopters deployed for river-flood rescues).
- Conflicting early counts across outlets in the first 24 hours: CNN (27 Sept) said "at least 4 dead, others missing"; Explorersweb's first report and Travel And Tour World said "10 missing"; other outlets said "14 missing." The Tourism Times and Explorersweb's follow-up settled on 16 total / 4 found / 12 missing by 28 Sept — treat 16/4/12 as the latest and most consistent figure.

Narration: Sixteen workers were buried at Himlung base camp on September twenty seventh, four found by the next day, twelve still missing.
