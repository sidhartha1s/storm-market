### Verdict: cleanest / highest-resolution / least-branded copy

**`ndtv-x-mountain-melts-into-river.mp4` is the cleanest edit** — no loop repeats, only one small corner logo and a single caption card that disappears after 5s (vs. permanent logos and rotating caption strips on the other two), and it's a longer continuous single take rather than a 3x-looped ~15s clip stretched to fill 37s. It is however the lowest native pixel count of the three (480x852, bitrate 656kbps, clearly an X/Twitter re-encode) — a genuine tradeoff, not a clean win. `wion-mountain-crashes-kaligandaki.mp4` and `ndtv-4-chilling-videos-mountains-crumbling.mp4` are both the same underlying vertical source pillarboxed into a 1920x1080 frame with blurred padding bars; nominally "1080p" but the sharp content area is a narrower strip that looks no more detailed than the X copy, just heavier on permanent branding (logo + rotating captions vs. logo + one card) and, for the WION file, padded out with 3x looping plus unrelated B-roll. **Recommendation: use `ndtv-x-mountain-melts-into-river.mp4` as the primary source for the collapse insert** (crop out the small NDTV bug top-right); fall back to the WION or NDTV-4 loop only if a shot of the debris more clearly reaching the water is needed, since neither of those actually shows a clean water impact either.
