And then the newsreel cameras arrived. This footage, shot for Fox Movietone News, is among the only motion picture ever taken of what the Tri-State Tornado left behind. It played in theaters across the country, and it did what the headlines could not. It made the scale real. Relief poured in. The Red Cross raised millions. Factories sent tractors and work crews. Tent cities rose beside the ruins, and the burying began, hundreds of funerals, town by town, week after week.
