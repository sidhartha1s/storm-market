And this storm had one more trick. It would not look like a tornado. It was wrapped in rain and cloud, a boiling black wall over a mile wide, sitting on the horizon like fog. People who had lived through twisters watched it come and did not run, because it did not match anything they knew. It was also moving at over sixty miles an hour, faster than almost any car on the roads that year. By the time the fog resolved into a funnel, it was already on top of you.
