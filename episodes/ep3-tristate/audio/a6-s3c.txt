Run the same afternoon today, and it looks like a different universe. The outlook would flag those exact counties days in advance. By morning, a tornado watch. As the storm crossed Missouri, radar would track its rotation minute by minute, and every phone from Ellington to Princeton would sound before it arrived. The storm would be just as violent. The difference is that the people of Gorham and Murphysboro and De Soto would have what their great grandparents were denied by policy. They would know it was coming.
