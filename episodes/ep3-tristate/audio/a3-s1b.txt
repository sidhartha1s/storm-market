The wreckage at Gorham told investigators what kind of wind this was. Wooden planks were found driven through the trunks of living trees, like nails through a board. A century later, one of those tree trunks still sits in a museum in Jackson County, with the plank still buried in it. Physics has a blunt explanation. To make soft pine pierce hardwood, the air around it has to be moving at speeds no building of that era could survive. Modern surveys rate this storm at the top of the damage scale. F five.
