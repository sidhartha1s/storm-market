Which leaves one practical lesson, and it has not changed in a century. A warning only works if you already know what you will do with it. The people who survived March eighteenth mostly survived by knowing, in the moment, where to go. Below ground beat above ground. A small interior room beat a wide open one. Away from windows beat next to them. Ten seconds of decision, made in advance, was the entire difference. That was true in nineteen twenty-five. It is exactly as true tonight.
