Griffin's schoolhouse came down with the rest of the town, and then, as in Murphysboro, fire found the wreckage. Some of the trapped could not be reached in time. It is one of the crueler patterns of this day. The storm passed a town in a minute, and then the stoves and lamps and gas lines it had scattered went to work on the ruins for hours. Survivors spoke of fighting two disasters, back to back, with the second one lit by the first.
