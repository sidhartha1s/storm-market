To feel how extreme that is, put it against everything else on record. The great Carolinas outbreak of nineteen eighty-four? Its longest continuous track ran about forty-two miles. The infamous Woodward, Oklahoma tornado of nineteen forty-seven, around a hundred. The Tri-State Tornado's most conservative, strictest, prove-it-or-drop-it measurement is one hundred fifty-one miles, and the honest measurement is far longer. A century of tornadoes, and second place is not even close.
