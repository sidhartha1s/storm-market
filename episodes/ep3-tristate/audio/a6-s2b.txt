And the storm left science a mystery it is still working on. Was the Tri-State Tornado truly one tornado, on the ground for two hundred nineteen miles? Or was it a family of tornadoes, spun up one after another by the same parent storm, with the gaps hidden in the rain? Modern researchers have walked the records mile by mile, and the honest answer is that the continuous track holds up remarkably well across most of the path. But parts of it may never be settled. Every witness who could answer the question is gone, and the storm, as always, left no photograph.
