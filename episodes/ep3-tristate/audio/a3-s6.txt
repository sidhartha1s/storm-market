Eleven minutes later, De Soto. A village of six hundred, and a two-story brick schoolhouse with the children still inside. The storm took the school's upper floor and brought it down into the classrooms below. Thirty-three children died in that building. It remains the deadliest thing a tornado has ever done to a single school in this country. In a village that small, there was no family untouched. Parents dug at the school with their bare hands while their own homes burned behind them. One survivor, a seven-year-old girl, was pulled from the rubble having lost three sisters at once.
