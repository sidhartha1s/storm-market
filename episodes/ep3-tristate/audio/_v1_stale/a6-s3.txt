The storm never stopped teaching. Decades later, Ted Fujita, the scientist who created the tornado damage scale, spent years reconstructing this storm's path mile by mile. Fujita had learned his forensic craft in the ruins of Hiroshima, reading destruction to understand the force that made it. He brought that same method to the fields of Illinois. And in December twenty twenty-one, a violent long-track tornado tore through Kentucky, along a corridor eerily close to this one. It was a monster. But this time there were warnings, minutes and even hours of them. And that is the difference between dozens of deaths, and hundreds.
