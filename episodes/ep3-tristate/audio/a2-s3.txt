For the next hour it marched northeast through farm country, and it grew. Witnesses stopped describing a funnel and started describing a wall. In places it was a mile wide. It shredded farmsteads that had stood since the Civil War. It threw debris so high that letters and photographs from Missouri later fell from the sky over Illinois, a hundred miles away. One country school was lifted and thrown, and somehow every child inside lived. Luck like that was about to run out.
