Two words went out
from the weather service
in Norman at 6:57.
Tornado emergency,
aimed at Moore
and the south side
of Oklahoma City.
The tornado had just
crossed Bridge Creek.
This statement was
aimed up the road.
If you had a
weather radio at home,
it sounded.
25 minutes later,
the tornado crossed
into Moore.
The agency's report
credits that phrase.
It added the emphasis
that prompted
numerous residents
to action.
