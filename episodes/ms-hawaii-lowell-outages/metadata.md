## Description (paste AFTER the channel's templated CTA block, already in YouTube settings)
One week after Hurricane Lowell passed west of Kauai, the Kauai Island Utility Cooperative has power back to 81 percent of its
members, with roughly 8,000 still out, almost all on the North Shore between Moloaa and Haena, and its chief executive will only
say clearer timing is coming in the next couple of days. The death toll has been revised to three, two on Kauai and one on Oahu,
after the county reported none a week ago. This update covers where the island stands section by section, why the last twenty
percent of the grid takes longest, the 100 outside crews flown in on an Air Force C-17, the congressional letter asking the
President for Major Disaster Declarations for both Lowell and Lala, and the numbers officials still have not given.
