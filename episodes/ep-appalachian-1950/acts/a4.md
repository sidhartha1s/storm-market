## A4 · The cold that went south
Chapter card:
Purpose · Pay Act 3's exit (the cold side did not stop in Ohio) by walking the record lows south from Chicago to the Gulf, then turn to what the whole storm cost people.
Question · How far south did the cold reach, and what did the storm cost?
Reveal · Chicago tied its all-time November low; Louisville and Lexington set November records that still stand, with Bowling Green's seven below now in a row of them; Asheville one, Atlanta three, Birmingham five, Pensacola twenty-two; crops froze in Georgia and South Carolina; the toll, at least a hundred sixty lives lost; a million homes dark; the costliest storm on record to that time.
Visual event · Self-made Southeast temperature map that drains blue from the Great Lakes to the Gulf, pin by pin, with Buffalo's orange "40s" pin left glowing at the top; on-screen source line for the toll.
Exit hook · Nobody working the forecast desks that weekend saw both sides coming (loop A4, paid in Act 5).
