## A5 · The storm the computer had to learn
Chapter card:
Purpose · Pay loop A4: show why nobody saw both sides coming, and how this storm became the test a computer forecast had to pass.
Question · Why didn't anyone see it coming, and could a machine?
Reveal · Hand-drawn forecasts could not anticipate it (AMS 2004); the starting maps showed only a weak ripple over Minnesota; the storm became the first test case for Charney's two-level computer model, which fell short, and that miss fed the start of daily computer forecasting in nineteen fifty-four; Norman Phillips wrote it up in nineteen fifty-eight.
