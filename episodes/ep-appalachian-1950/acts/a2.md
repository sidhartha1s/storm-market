## A2 · The side everybody remembers
Chapter card:
Purpose · Give the viewers their own snow memory back, then place it on the map so the snow side has a shape and a size next to Act 1's wind side.
Question · How much snow fell, and who was under it?
Reveal · Columbus pays loop A1 (wind chill minus eight, a one-day November snow record that still stands, a football game played inside it); Pittsburgh day by day; a band of thirty inches and more from Erie into West Virginia, Coburn Creek sixty-two; six point one million people under at least eighteen inches; the NCEI index ranks it Category Five, worst on record for the Ohio Valley.
Visual event · NWS Jackson KY snow map and the NCEI snowfall map, with a self-made band overlay from Erie to Coburn Creek; Snow Bowl film SOURCE TBD (fallback: self-made stadium graphic).
Exit hook · Paul on camera: one line on that Saturday's weather map explains Buffalo, Newark and Pittsburgh at once (cta-hinge, paid by Act 3's "The low went northwest.").
