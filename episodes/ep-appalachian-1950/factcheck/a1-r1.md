## Not settled
- Which station took New York City's 94 mph gust (row 27). Line is honest as written.
- The hour of Newark's 108 mph gust (row 55). "Same day" is the safe wording.
- New London 7.58 ft and the "only three storms higher" ranking, and Bridgeport 88 mph: one secondary source only (NBC CT); no NOAA gauge table fetched.
- Whether the jet explains Bear Mountain 140 or Newark 108: no source; only a general statement is supportable.
