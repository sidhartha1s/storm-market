## Headline findings
1. Formation date is a real conflict. NWS Jackson: developed on the 25th. Ocean Weather and gCaptain: secondary low deepened very rapidly over eastern North Carolina late on the 24th, near Washington DC by early morning of the 25th, then turned northwest across Pennsylvania. Wikipedia: formed morning of the 24th in southeast North Carolina (cites Ohio History). The script's "Saturday the twenty-fifth" and S5's "a full day before the big low even formed" cannot stand. S5 snow (Friday the 24th) was likely in the same window as formation, not a day earlier.
2. 980 mb and 1050 mb are secondary only (NBC CT "980ish", "1050ish near Maine"; Wikipedia 978 hPa uncited). Kept as "around/near". No primary found.
3. Albany (primary) names TWO highs: east of Labrador and over the Mississippi Valley. S8 says "that high".
4. Omega: NWS Charleston puts it in the upper-level pattern, 12Z 25 Nov, "starting to appear". Upper-air claim confirmed.
