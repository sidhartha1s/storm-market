## How the run goes
1. One clip per call, one take, 15 credits. The next call starts only after the last clip is checked. 6 clips = 90 credits.
2. Each downloaded clip is transcribed (whole clip, then each phrase between silences) and compared word for word with its line.
3. A speech defect a narrator take can cover is fixed with the narrator take of the same line. A clip is made again only when the picture is the problem or the defect sits on his mouth in full view.
4. Every clip gets the Warning Hour logo bug over Flow's watermark star (as ep4-el-reno).
