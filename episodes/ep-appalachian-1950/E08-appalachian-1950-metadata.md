📘 The book mentioned at 7:45

Saturday of Thanksgiving weekend, 1950. In Newark, New Jersey, the wind gusts to 108 miles an hour. That is hurricane force, and it is not a hurricane: it is a winter storm, and on its other side it is burying Pittsburgh under more than two feet of snow while Buffalo sits in the forties with no snow at all. This film follows both sides of the same storm at once: the coastal wind that hardly anyone remembers, the record snow and cold that everyone does, the left turn that put them together, the freeze that reached Pensacola, Florida, and the storm that early computer forecasting was later tested on.
