## Conflicts

1. Deaths: NWS JKL 160 and NCEI "at least 160" versus 383 (NOAA 20th-century list as quoted by usdeadlyevents; Wikipedia) versus 288-310 (state tally) versus about 300 (Smith MWR via Schwartz). The primary pages read (JKL, NCEI) both say 160.
2. Damage: $66.7M (Wikipedia, Nat'l Summary) vs $70M (NOAA list as relayed) vs $100M Northeast (AP). NWS/NCEI pages give no dollar figure.
3. NYC wind: JKL says 94 mph "New York City"; Mount Holly says Central Park 70 mph (strongest ever there). Two stations or two readings; the 94 mph is likely LaGuardia/other, unconfirmed.
4. Pittsburgh snow: 30.5 in (JKL), 30.2 in (NCEI), 27.4 in (NWS Pittsburgh records, 24-26 Nov). Different windows; use 27.4 in as the official 24-26 Nov record.
5. Atlanta low: 3F (NCEI) vs -3F (NBC CT, secondary).
6. Max snow: 57 in (JKL "as much as", Wikipedia Pickens) vs 62 in Coburn Creek (JKL, NCEI, RSI); JKL carries both.
7. Columbus: 14.9 in (Thu-Tue) vs 15.2 in (November total) on the same ILN page.
8. Storm dates: 22-30 Nov (NCEI), 24-29 Nov (RLX), 24-30 Nov (Wikipedia).
