## Section-wide decisions (each brief repeats the part it needs)
- **Cues.** Seconds from the segment's own start, on the spoken word (quoted). Multi-word cues land on the first word. Each comp is one segment, so comp time = word time (host data-start = segment start) unless the brief says otherwise.
- **A2-S2 words.json is broken.** `audio/a2-s2.words.json` holds only null bytes (read 28 Oct 26, 19:40). g-a2-2's cues come from a scratch faster-whisper base.en pass on the accepted `audio/a2-s2.wav` (12.12 s), not the pipeline file. The orchestrator re-transcribes a2-s2 with the pipeline and re-checks the 7 cues in g-a2-2 (expected drift under 0.1 s) before dispatch.
- **Corner circle.** Act 2 is narration with the presenter circle on wherever the corner is free (mg-quickref "Corner circle"). Every comp keeps x >= 1600, y >= 680 free of labels, numbers, pins and highlighted shapes for its whole window.
- **L1 and cta-hinge stay open.** No comp in this act draws a storm track, a low ("L"), a front, isobars, a pressure number, wind arrows, or any line, band or shading that joins the snow side to the coast side. The A2 snow band (g-a2-7) is a snow-amount band inside PA/OH/WV, not a track. Act 3 owns the mechanism ("The low went northwest.").
- **Popfig rule.** Red Abril popfigs sit only over footage, never over a card or map comp. So "THANKSGIVING NIGHT" (A2-S2) becomes the card's headline in g-a2-2, not a popfig; the only A2 popfig is the Snow Bowl date over the still in g-a2-4.
- **Source lines.** Every comp carries its Picture-line source as a corner credit: Arial 700 19 px `#c8d3e0` tracking 2, at left 110, bottom 60 (never the right corner, the circle sits there). It is on from the comp's first beat to its exit.
- **One map, one coordinate system** (same as `briefs/intro/_list.md`, so the A2 maps and the hook map share one basemap): map x = 3841 × (lon + 97.576), map y = -5585 × (lat - 46.6213); screen px = (map unit - frame top-left) × s. North up, no rotation. Basemap credit "Basemap: Natural Earth" sits at the source-line spot; on map comps the source line goes on the line above it (bottom 90).
