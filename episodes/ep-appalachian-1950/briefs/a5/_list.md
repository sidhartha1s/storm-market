## Section-wide decisions (each brief repeats the part it needs)
- **No Paul clip in A5.** All nine segments are narration (`paul-clips.md` names no A5 clip), so no popfig-on-Paul rule applies. The corner circle may run: every comp keeps x >= 1600, y >= 680 free of labels, numbers, chips and highlighted shapes.
- **Source lines** sit bottom-left (left 110, bottom 60, Arial 700 20 px `#c8d3e0`, glass pill), never at `.srclab`'s default bottom-right, which is the circle corner. Map comps fold the basemap credit into the same line.
- **Popfig limits** (big line 16 characters or fewer, 3 words or fewer): "1950 · FORECAST BY HAND" becomes big "1950" + sub "FORECAST BY HAND"; "PRINCETON, NEW JERSEY" becomes big "PRINCETON" + sub "NEW JERSEY"; the S6 "date card" is a popfig, big "1954" + sub "JOINT NUMERICAL WEATHER PREDICTION UNIT" (dates are never a colour-block card). Popfigs never sit over a map comp: S1's popfig plays while the chart is still near black, S6's after the slab has cleared.
- **No date on the starting map** (fact-check a5-r2 rows 12, 17, 28): the Minnesota wave is the start state of a modern reanalysis re-forecast, undated, and not stated to be the Charney test's start. g-a5-3 and g-a5-5 show no date, no "24 NOV", and g-a5-5 does not reuse g-a5-3's ripple as its input.
- **S4 photo** is the IAS machine in 1952 with Oppenheimer and von Neumann in front. A "JULE CHARNEY" name card over their faces would name the wrong men, so the comp crops to the machine only; the 1950 one-day forecast ran on ENIAC, so the photo is captioned with its own year (1952) in the source line.
- **Segment offsets inside multi-segment comps** assume a 0.40 s gap between narration segments (no A5 index.html yet). `SEG` tables in g-a5-5 and g-a5-8 are replaced by the real `data-start` differences; every cue is `SEG[seg] + word time`.
- **Projection** (same as the intro maps): map x = 3841 × (lon + 97.576), map y = -5585 × (lat - 46.6213); screen = (map - frame top-left) × s. All positions in the map briefs were computed by script (python3, 28 Oct 26), never by eye.
- **Track and mounts.** Full-frame comps on track 2; g-a5-9-title on track 3 over g-a5-8.
