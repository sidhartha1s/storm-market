There was no seawall. What stood between the Gulf and the rest of the town was its own wreckage. It piled up four to six blocks in from the beach. By eight PM, drifting houses had lodged against his, and the waves made them a battering ram. The barometer had fallen about an inch since noon. At eight thirty PM it hit bottom, and his house went down. About fifty people were inside it. Only eighteen of them came through. His wife, Cora, was pregnant. Isaac wrote that she never rose above the water after the house broke apart.
