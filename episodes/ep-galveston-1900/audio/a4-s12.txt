He had passed out in the water. When he came around, he was holding his youngest child. Five minutes later Joseph reached him with the other two. They pulled a woman and a child from the water, and all of them drifted on the wreckage for three hours. They climbed off near eleven thirty PM, only about three hundred yards from where they started. When daylight came, the Gulf was nearly back to normal. And what did the daylight show?
