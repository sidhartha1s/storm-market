## Changes from the Picture lines (the orchestrator decides; each brief says why)
1. A2-S1 popfig "HAVANA · CUBA" becomes the map pin label "Havana, Cuba": popfigs carry dates and figures only and never sit over a map comp (mg-quickref "Palette and surfaces"; the intro made the same change).
2. "Galveston island graphic" (A2-S1, S4, S7) is the Galveston pin and label on the shared plate, as in g-intro-4: the island cross-section (g-intro-6) is a different scale and cannot sit on a Gulf map.
3. A2-S4 "Havana close-up with two pins": no research row places either office on a street (Belen's address and the Bureau's office are unsourced), so the close-up is a round inset card at Havana with the two pins side by side and no street or coast detail inside it.
4. A2-S6 Cuba's government telegraph lines: no research row gives the network. Drawn as one schematic line along the island, Havana to Santiago de Cuba, labelled "Schematic".
5. A2-S7: a dotted "Letters" line Belen to Washington is added on "Willis Moore" (rows 23, 25: letters both ways, contents unseen). It carries "So the Americans could hear him" and fills a 6 s stretch with no picture event. Drop it if the orchestrator prefers the Picture line as written.
6. A2-S8 Belen arrow: the Picture line has it "aimed across the water at the Galveston pin". Research supports only "northwest, into the Gulf" (sources-cuba.md Conflict 3; g-intro-4 truth 2), so the arrow ends in the open Gulf, at least 250 px short of the Texas coast, and never points at Galveston.
7. A2-S8 chip "RUBIERA: 'STRENGTHEN IN THE GULF'" loses its quote marks: Rubiera wrote in Spanish ("se fortalecería", "mucha fuerza en el Golfo"); the English is a gloss (a2-r1 row 51), not a quotation.

## Shared rules (every brief in this folder points here)
### Words and times
- Every cue is seconds from the start of that segment's wav, read from `audio/a2-s<n>.words.json`. Whisper writes digits ("1870.", "1898,", "4th,", "30") and misspells names: Viñes is "Venez"/"Venus's"/"Venez's", Gangoiti is "Gangu what"/"Ganguetti"/"gangoide", Belen is "Bellen" once. On screen it is always Viñes, Gangoiti, Belen.
- Wav lengths (s, measured): S1 21.062, S2 31.898, S4 24.092, S5 17.672, S6 17.978, S7 20.517, S8 17.904, S9 8.321.
- Host `data-start` = that segment's `nx-a2-s<n>` `data-start` in index.html plus the brief's first cue. A comp that ends at its segment's end ends at the wav length above, not at the last word.
- The act is locked and graded; if a segment is ever re-voiced, retime from the new words.json, never by offset.
