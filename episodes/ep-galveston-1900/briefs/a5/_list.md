Popfig 0.16 to 4.60; the film starts at "In" 7.54 ("In the days after").
2. A5-S1 chip "FILMED IN THE RUINS DAYS LATER · ©SEPT 24, 1900" is a two-line chip: main "FILMED IN THE RUINS DAYS LATER", tag "© SEPT 24, 1900". Words unchanged.
3. A5-S3 "the 1891 page and the MWR report page slide in, side by side": the narration's "two accounts Cline left of his own Saturday" are the report and the later ride telling (fact-check a5-r1 row 15), not the 1891 article. g-a5-4 shows the MWR report and the TSHA retelling. The 1891 page would credit the wrong document.

7. A5-S6 adds a quote card for the Salon line (6.84 to 13.90), which the narration paraphrases and the Picture line leaves without a visual. Text is Larson's own words, fact-check a5-r1 row 25.

## Shared rules (A5 deltas on top of `briefs/intro/_list.md` "Shared rules")
- Cue times are seconds from the start of that segment's wav (`audio/a5-s<n>.words.json`). Whisper writes "Klein" for Cline, "Eric" for Erik, "stockmen" for Stockman, "Dario" for Diario, "Bellin" for Belen, "everyone" for "every one", "Texas'" for Texas's, digits for numbers ("6 ,000", "24th", "1945."). On screen the right spelling always.
- Segment-to-host time, placement (source line top-left, lower-third, chip, popfig), drift and one element per zone: exactly as `briefs/intro/_list.md` "Shared rules".
- Presenter circle: on in this act wherever the corner x >= 1600, y >= 680 holds only footage or empty ground. Every brief keeps that corner empty of text; full-frame maps and diagrams state whether the circle must go off.
- Document pages (MWR scans, typeset TSHA): whole lines only (mg-quickref "Text doctrine", last bullet). A "lit" line = a highlight bar `rgba(216,161,58,.32)` (Amber 1 at 32 %) behind the line's measured box, `scaleX 0 -> 1` from the left over 0.5 s power2.out, while the rest of the page dims to opacity 0.55.
- Every on-screen number has a research row named in its brief §1.
- 6. CONTRACT, 7. TOOLS, 7a. DONE LINE, 8. RECEIPT: as `references/mg-brief-template.md` sections 6 to 8, word for word, COMPID = the brief's comp id, slug `ep-galveston-1900`, root id `<comp id>-root`. Edit only your own `compositions/<comp id>.html`.
