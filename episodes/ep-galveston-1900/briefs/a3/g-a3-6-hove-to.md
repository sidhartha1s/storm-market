## 0. Claim
Halsey guessed the wind at more than 100 mph; they hove to from noon to 3 p.m.; his was the only vessel known to have met the storm in the Gulf, which the Bureau took as a sign its warnings were generally observed at Gulf ports.

## 1. Source
MWR Sept 1900 p. 374 (factcheck/a3-r1.md rows 16 to 19): "I think the wind was blowing at the rate of more than 100 miles an hour"; "we hove to from 12 to 3 o'clock"; "this is the only vessel that is known to have encountered the storm in the Gulf of Mexico indicates that the Weather Bureau warnings and advices were generally observed at Gulf ports".
