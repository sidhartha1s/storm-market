# Fact-check a4-r1: acts/a4.md (A4-S1 to A4-S12), 28 Oct 26
Sources: Cline report, Monthly Weather Review Sept 1900 (OCR `scratch/agentpatterns/batchA/a3gal/mwr.txt`, checked against the PDF `media/candidates/intro/H1-S5_c1_mwr-sep1900-vol28-no9.pdf`, PDF pages 2 to 4); sources-cline.md; factcheck/a3-r1, a3-r2; references/flag-words.md; Wikipedia 1900 Galveston hurricane (SECONDARY).

## Page ruling
PDF page 3 header, viewed at 200 dpi: "373". The OCR "378" is a misread. So: swells, telegram, barometer one-tenth, rain "about noon" = p. 372. Wind, 84 mph, 120 mph, barometer 29.42 and 28.48, warnings, Strand, 3:30 line, house, 7:30 rise, 8:30 collapse = p. 373 (one page, the page that begins "between northwest and northeast"). Drift, landing, "following morning" = p. 374 (the page starting "and a woman and child"). Source lines to fix: S6 (rain at noon is on p. 372) and S7 ("pp. 373 and 378" must read "p. 373").

## House count ruling
Cline: "about fifty persons"; "all but eighteen were hurled into eternity" = about 32 lost, 18 survived. Wikipedia (SECONDARY, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1900_Galveston_hurricane): "became a refuge for 50 people during the storm but collapsed in the general wreck, killing 32 of its occupants including Mrs. Cline herself." The two agree: 50 in, 32 lost, 18 out. NOT a conflict. S11 spoken line "about fifty people inside. Only eighteen of them came through." stands. The "32" is the number lost, not the number inside.
