## New evidence for research
- Cline's own count, MWR Sept 1900 (archive.org URL above): "conservative estimates place the loss of life at the appalling figure, 6,000." The words "at least 6,000 people were killed in a few hours' time" are NOAA Heritage's intro (https://vlab.noaa.gov/web/nws-heritage/-/galveston-storm-of-1900), not Cline's.
- AOML: "Only the Weather Bureau prognostication was carried over the wires." (supports I1-S3 and the H1-S4 telegraph graphic).
- MWR, Capt. Halsey: "We were about half way across the Gulf when the storm center passed us." Wikipedia: "In the eastern Gulf of Mexico on September 6, the ship Louisiana encountered the hurricane." (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1900_Galveston_hurricane, SECONDARY).
- Wikipedia Isaac Cline (SECONDARY): "chief meteorologist at the Galveston, Texas, office ... from 1889 to 1901"; brother Joseph at Galveston from 1892.
- NPS has no "warned" sentence (checked this round); T1-S1 "warned" rests on TSHA and MWR.
