## A4 · Saturday
Chapter card: Purpose · walk Saturday the eighth hour by hour through Cline's own report, from the dawn swells to the house going down, so the city's expert lives the storm his 1891 article ruled out.
Chapter card: Question · the warnings were out and the water was in the streets by afternoon, so how high would it get at Cline's own house, on ground at an elevation of five point two feet?
Chapter card: Reveal · about twenty feet of tide at his door, four feet of it in about four seconds; the barometer bottomed at 28.48 inches at 8:30 p.m., the minute his house went down with about fifty people in it, Cora among the lost.
Chapter card: Visual event · the MWR page with the 3:30 line lit; the barograph trace (MWR fig. 1) drawing its plunge; a self-made water-depth bar on the house at Twenty-fifth and Q climbing past the 5.2 ft ground line to 15.2 ft and then about 20 ft.
Chapter card: Exit hook · they came ashore near half past eleven, three hundred yards from where they started; by Sunday morning the Gulf was nearly normal, and daylight would show what it had done (A5: the films of the ruins).
