## How the run goes
1. One clip per call, one take, 15 credits. The next call starts only after the last clip is checked. 6 clips = 90 credits.
2. Each downloaded clip is transcribed (whole clip, then each phrase between silences) and compared word for word with its line.
3. A speech defect a narrator take can cover is fixed with the narrator take of the same line. A clip is made again only when the picture is the problem or the defect sits on his mouth in full view.
4. Every clip gets the Warning Hour logo bug over Flow's watermark star (as ep4-el-reno).

## Same for every clip
- Mode: frames to video, start frame = the desk frame of @char1 already used for the book ad. One 10 second video, single take.
- The character is called @char1 in the prompt, never by name.
- Prompt body: `This is a history documentary about the 1900 Galveston hurricane. @char1 sits at his desk and speaks straight to the camera. Medium shot, the camera does not move. Calm, deep, measured voice, natural small hand movement. He says exactly these words and nothing else: "<LINE>" He stops speaking and holds his look at the camera. No music, no captions, no text on screen.`
