## Description

On April 3, 1974, the United States recorded seven F5 tornadoes in a single day. One hundred and forty eight
tornadoes crossed thirteen states in eighteen hours, and three hundred and thirty people died. The man who ran
the national warning office had seen it coming the day before. This is what a day's warning was worth in 1974,
and what the same ground looks like now.
