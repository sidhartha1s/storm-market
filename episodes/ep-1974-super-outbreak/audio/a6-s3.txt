Gus Allen still lives near Brandenburg, and his sister, his brother and his small cousin are on the memorial there. It was just a bad day, he said, and the same ground has already taken another one. Pearson knew a day early, and the warnings he set up saved the people who heard them, and in nineteen seventy four hearing one meant a radio that happened to be switched on. Today those minutes get you to a storm shelter or an inside room on the lowest floor, and they do not get a car past a mile wide tornado. Know before the day comes which room in your house is the one you run to.
