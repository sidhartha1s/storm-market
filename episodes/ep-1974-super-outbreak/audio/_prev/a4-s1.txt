At six twenty P M, Central time, a tornado formed near Moulton, Alabama, and stayed on the ground for fifty two miles. Twenty eight people died along that first tornado's fifty two miles, from Lawrence County into Limestone, and the neighbors were out in the wreckage behind it pulling people out when the second one came. It formed about an hour after the first, ran a mile to the west of it for ninety eight miles, and honestly it went straight through the people doing the rescuing. So two F five tornadoes hit one small town an hour apart. To this day nobody can say for certain which one killed whom, because the people who could have told you were the ones under the second one. Between the two of them, sixteen people died in Limestone County alone, and about fifty across that stretch of north Alabama.
