That same evening, up north, one tornado came down near Otterbein, Indiana, and didn't lift for a hundred and twenty one miles. At five seventeen P M, local time, it went through the middle of Monticello, where the county courthouse came apart and a safe on the third floor fell straight through to the first. A railroad bridge over the Tippecanoe River was lifted off its footings and twisted. Rod Poole, who later ran the town's water department, said the sky was a greenish brown, and that everybody says a tornado sounds like a train, and they're right. Eighteen people died along those hundred and twenty one miles.
