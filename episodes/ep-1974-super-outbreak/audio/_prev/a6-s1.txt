Put another map on top of the nineteen seventy four tracks. On April twenty seventh, twenty eleven, an EF five tornado came down at Hackleburg, Alabama, and ran a hundred and thirty two miles across five counties, and it killed seventy two people, the deadliest single tornado in the state's history. In Limestone County it ran straight down the nineteen seventy four Tanner corridor. It destroyed homes, a mobile home park and a church that the first two Tanner tornadoes had destroyed thirty seven years earlier, and that people had rebuilt on the same ground. A weather service meteorologist and a geography professor had been comparing the tracks for three years before it happened, and afterward the local paper quoted them saying the paths matched almost exactly. The second time, the numbers went the other way.
