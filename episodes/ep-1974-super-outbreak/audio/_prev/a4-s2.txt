At eight twenty five P M a tornado touched down in Mississippi and didn't lift for over two hours, a hundred and one miles, one of the longest-lived violent tornadoes ever recorded. It was heading for Guin, Alabama, a town of about two thousand five hundred people, and it got there in full dark, about two hours after sunset. A young man named Allen Lindley put a tape recorder in an open window, and he got a minute of it coming and eighteen seconds of it going over the house. I have the thing on tape, he said, and it's a rare recording of an F five passing directly over a house. Becky Todd was in the car with her family when it rolled, and she said George told them to grab the seats and hold on. Her sons Mark and Paul didn't survive. Seven hundred and thirty seven houses were destroyed in Guin that night, and twenty eight people died.
