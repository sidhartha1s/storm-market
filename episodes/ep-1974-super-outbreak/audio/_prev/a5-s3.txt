And the map answered the first question, which is how one day makes seven of them. You take warm, wet air off the Gulf of Mexico, put a layer of dry air over the top of it, run a jet stream above that to turn the whole column, and send a cold front through to set it off. That ran from Indiana to Alabama, all afternoon and all night. Every storm that formed in that column had the same engine. One machine made all of them, and it ran for the better part of a day.
