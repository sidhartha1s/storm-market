Dayton's weather office put a tornado warning on Xenia at four ten P M, Eastern time, which gave the town thirty minutes, and in nineteen seventy four thirty minutes was a gift. Xenia still became the worst-hit town of the whole day. At four:thirty three a funnel came down near Lower Bellbrook Road, and at four:forty it was in the middle of the city, half a mile to a mile wide. It went through Windsor Park and Arrowhead, streets full of the houses people had bought after the war.
