So by seven thirty that evening the neighbours were out in the wreckage with flashlights pulling people out, and that is when the second one came. It formed about an hour after the first, ran a mile to the west of it for ninety eight miles, and it went straight through the people doing the rescuing. So two F five tornadoes hit one small town an hour apart, and to this day nobody can say for certain which one killed whom, because the people who could have told you were the ones under the second one.
