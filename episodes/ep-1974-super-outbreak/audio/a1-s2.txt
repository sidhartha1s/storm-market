The tornado that hit Brandenburg, Kentucky, formed at three thirty P M, Central time, and the town had fifteen minutes of warning. The tornado came up the Ohio River toward the town so low and so wide that people said it looked like a wall, and it was half a mile across. A hundred and twenty eight homes and thirty businesses were gone from a town of sixteen hundred people, and thirty one people were dead along its path. Fifteen minutes is enough to get below ground, and at Brandenburg a hundred and twenty eight homes were levelled or swept away anyway. A boy named Tim Haley, he was fifteen. He spent that night carrying people out of the wreckage, a hundred and sixty nine of them by his own count. One of them was a baby he had to take from a mother's arms so he could get her to safety. He became a soldier later, and fifty years on he called that night pretty, pretty traumatic.
