He was grading by what was left. A well built house with its walls gone is an F four. The same house swept clean off its foundation, with the cars a hundred yards away and the trees stripped of their bark, is an F five. In Guin, a man named Robert Pennington came home from his shift at the electric company to that. He said there was no sign of the house, the foundation, the framing, or anything, and his wife Juanita, who was sixty four, was found dead. Fujita read ten thousand miles of that from the air, and seven times he wrote the top of his scale.
