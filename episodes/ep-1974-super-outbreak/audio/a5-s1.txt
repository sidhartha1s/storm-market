Ted Fujita had invented his scale in nineteen seventy one, and it graded a tornado from zero to five by the damage it left, because nobody could measure the wind inside one. He had a hundred and forty eight tornadoes to grade at once, so he flew. Ten months, more than ten thousand miles, low and slow, with every track drawn by hand onto one map. And somewhere in those ten thousand miles he found a kind of damage that no tornado could have made.
