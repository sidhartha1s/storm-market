The first F five of the day came down south of Huffman, Indiana, at two sixteen P M, Central time, and for sixty two miles it stayed over farmland, through six counties and never a town. Six people died, in farmhouses that happened to be in the way. An hour and a quarter later a town with a warning, with fifteen minutes to get ready, would lose twenty eight people, and thirty one along the whole path. The warning reached that town in time, and it still lost twenty eight people, and the reason is what was under the houses.
