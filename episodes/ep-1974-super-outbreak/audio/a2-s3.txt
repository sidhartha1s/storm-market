Thirty four people died along that path, thirty two of them inside the city, and about twelve hundred were hurt. Fourteen hundred buildings were damaged or gone, and five schools were destroyed: Xenia High, Central Junior High, McKinley, Simon Kenton and Arrowood. Xenia got its thirty minutes, and the tornado still took five schools, fourteen hundred buildings and thirty four people, because thirty minutes moves you across a room, not out of a city. That same evening Governor John Gilligan sent two hundred and fifty National Guardsmen into Xenia, and by midnight fourteen hundred and forty five were on duty across Ohio, more than half of them in this one town. They stayed for weeks, helping with the rescue and the clean up, and standing guard over what was left of the houses on Windsor Park and Arrowhead.
