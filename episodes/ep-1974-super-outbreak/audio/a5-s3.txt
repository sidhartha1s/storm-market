And the map answered the first question, which is how one day makes seven of them. You take warm, wet air off the Gulf of Mexico, put a layer of dry air over the top of it, run a jet stream above that to turn the whole column, and send a cold front through to set it off. That set up stretched from Indiana to Alabama, and it held all afternoon and all night. The storms came up in three long lines, and every one of them ran on the same engine.
