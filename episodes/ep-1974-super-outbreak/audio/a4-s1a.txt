At about six thirty P M, Central time, a tornado came down in north Alabama, and it stayed on the ground county after county, all the way from Lawrence into Limestone. Twenty eight people died along that first tornado's path, and the neighbors were out in the wreckage behind it pulling people out when the second one came. It formed about an hour after the first, ran a mile to the west of it for ninety eight miles, and honestly it went straight through the people doing the rescuing.
