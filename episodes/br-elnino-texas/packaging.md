## Net-info-gain (facts no competitor video carries; primary sources)
- NWS Houston/Galveston, Storm Signals vol. 47: winter 1997–98 produced **over 300 severe / tornado / flash-flood
  warnings from November to February**, against 540 for all of 1997 — "nearly 3 times as many warnings" as a
  normal winter. Houston Hobby's 1997 total of **79.97 inches** was the third-highest year on record.
- NWS Houston: the **January 6, 1998 flood** dropped 10–12 inches; Lake Houston crested at **47.48 ft against a
  44.5 ft spillway**.
- NWS Fort Worth: the **December 26, 2015** outbreak (strong El Niño winter) — 12 tornadoes, 13 dead, the
  Garland–Rowlett **EF4**, the first EF4 ever recorded in Texas in December (Wikipedia/Tornado Talk; NWS ranks it
  #4 for December tornado counts in 24 h).
- NOAA CPC Oct–Dec 2026 outlook (issued Aug 20, 2026): Texas sits in the **"Above" precipitation** band; the
  temperature map has Texas at **"Equal Chances"** (checked on the map itself, not assumed).
