## Description
https://paulraymond.online
📘 The book mentioned at 3:21 — The Storm-Ready Home

Kauai's power number did not move overnight: 81 percent restored, about 5,000 accounts still dark, all of them on one damaged
transmission line along the North Shore. Almost everything under that number did move. Nearly every public school is open
again, the two North Shore elementary schools are targeted to reopen today, the harbors are taking cargo, resident damage
reports have passed 550, and Hawaii's four members of Congress have put their letter on the President's desk.

This update covers: where the restoration stands (KIUC's 81 percent, 1,850 customers back on Sunday, about 100 outside line
workers, six utility trucks flown in by the Air Force), the North Shore corridor (Kuhio Highway on local traffic only, the
Kalihiwai and Wainiha bridges, Kilauea and Hanalei elementary), the harbors (Nawiliwili cargo only, Port Allen open, Kaumalapau
on Lanai at limited capacity), the record (David Bissell's transmission timeline, the delegation's letter, Governor Green's
$400 million estimate), why one transmission line decides the North Shore, the federal track (a Major Disaster Declaration,
the $67 million figure that belongs to Lala, five disasters in six months), and what nobody has said yet.
