## THIN CATEGORIES (honest gaps)
- **USCG/dispatch radio traffic audio**: no publicly released recording located. Rescue-swimmer/pilot first-hand accounts exist in the USCG "Darkest Day" monograph (#11) but as prose, not audio.
- **NOAA Weather Radio audio of the Aug 28 bulletin**: only the text bulletin (#31, #32) was found; no confirmed audio recording of an actual radio broadcast.
- **FOIA'd or news-published full 911 call libraries beyond CBS's 2015 release**: CBS's "Voices in the Storm" (#46) appears to be the main released set; a broader/complete 911 tape archive was not located.
- **DVIDS direct API/search**: DVIDS has no public search API without an account; all DVIDS entries here came from web search, not a systematic site crawl — a follow-up pass logged into DVIDS directly would likely surface more USCG/DoD assets.
