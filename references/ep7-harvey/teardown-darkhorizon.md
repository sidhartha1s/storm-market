## a. HOOK ANATOMY (0:00–0:26)

No flash-forward device here (contrast with Dark Horizon's own Katrina video, which opens on an out-of-order Nagin press-conference quote). Instead the Harvey video opens with **in-scene vox-pop panic audio, chronologically in order**:
- **0:00–0:14** — Cold open, real-sounding first-person quote over what is presumably coastal prep b-roll: *"We got sand in sandbags because everywhere was out with sandbags. We have a total of 40."* Immediately followed by a second panic-buying quote: *"The essentials you need to get by vanishing from store shelves... people were actually fighting over a loaf of bread... yelling and screaming in the middle of the floor over this two loaves of bread."* This is the hook — human panic and scarcity, not storm stats.
- **0:14–0:26** — Pivot to the meteorological setup: "Meteorologists had been watching a disturbance in the Gulf of Mexico for days... a disorganized cluster of thunderstorms, a remnant low that had barely survived its trek across the Caribbean."

No spoken thesis/foreshadow line lands in the 60–90s window the way "it drowned an entire state" did in the Katrina video — the title's own claim ("almost ended Texas") is never asserted in narration at all, only implied. This is a notable difference from Dark Horizon's own Katrina formula and reads as a weaker hook mechanically, though the panic-audio cold open is still an effective real-audio-first opener.

## c. COVERAGE LIST

**What it covers:**
- Full meteorological timeline: Gulf of Campeche warm-water fuel, rapid intensification to Cat 4, Rockport/San Jose Island landfall at ~130+ mph
- Rockport/coastal-bend destruction (marinas, stripped trees, sheared building tops)

- Final toll: rainfall record, structures flooded, $125B cost tying Katrina

**What it does NOT cover (confirmed gap — both by transcript absence and by direct audience pushback, see comment-insights.md):**
- **The Addicks/Barker reservoir controlled-release decision itself.** The video names the reservoirs once, in passing, as "filling... approaching the limits of the spillways" — it never explains that the Army Corps made a deliberate choice to open the gates, that this was a known, calculated decision to flood homes on purpose, or that it happened on a specific date/time. This is the single largest gap and it is exactly the audience's loudest complaint (see comment-insights.md #1).
- Flood-pool development — homes built inside the reservoir pools themselves, sold by developers who knew the land was designed to flood
- The 2019 federal court ruling (Fifth Amendment takings case, property owners vs. US government) — zero mention
- FEMA flood-map failure (per the StormForge video's own reporting, 75% of homes that flooded during Harvey were outside the mapped 100-year flood zone) — zero mention
- Houston Mayor Sylvester Turner's decision NOT to order a mass evacuation, and the 2005 Hurricane Rita precedent (100+ traffic deaths) that drove that call
- Named, personal survivor vignettes — every human moment in this video is an anonymous vox-pop quote, never a named individual with a followed arc
- The post-storm buyout program, the 2018 Harris County flood-control bond, or any environmental-justice/inequality angle in the recovery
- Any legal/accountability throughline at all — this video is a pure storm-and-rescue narrative with zero "who is responsible" framing

## e. RETENTION MECHANICS

- **Cut rate / pacing:** Fast, dense narration throughout with almost no slow "explainer holds" — even the meteorology-explainer beat (training effect) is delivered in a single quick analogy rather than a sustained graphics-heavy segment.
- **Hook device:** Real-sounding panic-buying vox pop, chronologically first, not a flash-forward. No planted open loop and no thesis/foreshadow line — this is a structurally weaker hook than Dark Horizon's own Katrina formula (no "what happened next..." payoff moment).
- **Rehook cadence:** Roughly every 45–90s there's an escalation beat: a new stat, a broadcast-style quote, or a scene change (evacuation prep → intensification → landfall → stall → rescue → toll). This tracks close to the channel's usual rehook rhythm.
- **Ending:** Soft, reflective community-themed close ("the way we hold each other, that remains") with no survivor callback and no cliffhanger to another video — straight to a generic subscribe ask. No mid-roll CTA of any kind (no ad break, no "subscribe" mid-video, no next-video tease).
- **Structural weakness vs. its own Katrina video:** the Katrina teardown noted an open-loop mechanic (levee failure teased early, paid off late). This Harvey video has no equivalent open loop — the reservoir mention at 9:20 is never set up as a mystery or paid off; it simply passes by as ambient detail. This is a real structural miss relative to the channel's own established formula, and it is the exact gap our accountability-first angle can exploit.
