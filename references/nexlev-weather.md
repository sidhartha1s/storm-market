## Top 3 recommendations

1. **Tornado/storm history documentaries** (model: Disasters Explained, SkyWhisper, Dark Horizon) — highest and most consistent outlier multiples (50-130x) with low production complexity (archive footage + narration), and the topic well never runs dry given decades of US tornado/hurricane history.
2. **Weather science/forecast explainers** (model: Mr. Weatherman Asia) — least crowded of the group, evergreen educational angle usually pulls premium RPM, and titles skew toward current/ongoing storms so it rides real-time search demand.
3. **Extreme-cold survival narrative** (model: Frostline Chronicles, ArcticSurvival) — the single biggest multiples in the dataset (up to 257x) because it blends weather with human-survival storytelling, a proven high-retention YouTube format, though it requires slightly higher-effort scripting than pure disaster narration.
