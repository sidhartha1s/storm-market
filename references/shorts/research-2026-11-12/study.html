Where to take Paul's reels: five directions, in order

Nothing here is built. Every storm fact in these lines still has to come from an episode fact sheet.

1 The Warning Hour, played by the viewer copies oddhourtales + dontopenit.stories

A real storm, told as a serial where the viewer is in it. Frame one: a clock, the date, the town, a phone alert. Rules on a card are the real safety rules for that storm. Each reel ends on the choice real people faced (stay or drive, basement or bathtub, leave the island or wait). Comments vote. The next reel opens 'Most of you chose to drive' and shows what happened to the people who did, from the record. Paul is the voice on the radio that cuts in with the forecast: the brand is present in every reel without a face on frame one.

Why first: it is the only format here where every recent reel is large (the two serial accounts' medians are the two highest in the table), the name of the channel already describes it, and the vote is a comment, which is what the comment-to-DM funnel needs. Nobody found here is doing it with true events.

2 Right now, above you copies charles.zhengg

One storm object followed end to end with a counter in the corner. Frame-one title names something of the viewer's: 'THE HAIL THAT HITS YOUR CAR TONIGHT is 9 km up right now'. The counter runs (height, temperature, seconds to the ground). Paul appears once, for two seconds, in the middle.

Why second: two reels in 45 days at 119x and 157x the account's median, and the format is a pure animation job. Needs a real number at every step.

3 One question about your own body or house in weather copies zhscience

'Why do your ears pop before a storm?' 'Why does the sky go green?' 20–30 s, flat illustration, a study in the middle, answer at the end.

Why third: cheapest to make daily; 3.0M on an account with 2,607 followers. Lowest ceiling for the brand because nothing in it is ours.

4 Paul in the box (Kallaway's layout) copies kallawaymarketing

Title on top, a diagram that draws itself in the middle, Paul in a rounded box on the bottom third, one-word captions. Script on Kallaway's skeleton: outcome line, named method, steps with a reason to stay at each change.

Use for authority pieces and for the giveaway reels. Kallaway's own median is 53K on 240K followers: it converts (thousands of comments), it does not spread like the three above.

5 Storm Town (wild card) copies realm.of.many, bent

Every follower gets a house with their handle in one animated town. Each Friday a real historical storm hits the town at its real strength; houses whose owners commented the right preparation that week are standing on Saturday.

Untested anywhere. It turns the follow into a stake in the story. One reel of realm.of.many did 277K; its median is 8K, so the mechanic alone is not enough.
