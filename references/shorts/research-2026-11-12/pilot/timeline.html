The three parts

1. Stay or Go (this one). You are in a mobile home in Grady County at 6:26 PM. Hallway or the neighbor's cellar.

2. Drive or Shelter. Opens "Most of you chose…", then the record: thirty-five people in one cellar, the door pulled open, every one came up alive; in that same county twelve people didn't survive, most of them in mobile homes; near that tornado, FEMA's engineers found anchor straps that had broken. Then 6:57 PM: the first Tornado Emergency, Moore is 25 minutes ahead, you are in a house with no basement, and one TV station is urging people to get out of the path. Drive or shelter.

3. Gym or Hallway. The record on the road: two women lost their lives under highway overpasses. Then Westmoore High, an awards ceremony, several hundred students and parents, the tornado within a hundred yards. You are the assistant principal. Ends on what the school did, and sends people to the full film.

What makes it ours, not the same animation as everyone

  • It happened. Every choice is one real people faced, and the answer comes from the weather service's own report, shown as the page with the line highlighted.
  • Real pictures: the tornado photos by NOAA and the weather service, the real damage path map. Drawings only where no camera was.
  • Everything on screen is from 1999: a tube TV, a weather radio, a wall clock. No phone alerts, because there were none.
  • Paul. His face in the red circle and his voice, in every reel.
