## How the story is told (timestamps from the transcript)
- **0:00 cold open.** It opens in second person, present tense, at the moment of impact ("It's April 14th, 1912,
  11:30 p.m. You're standing on the deck"). Then it states the paradox (the clearest night, yet no one saw a
  200 ft berg) and lists the official blame (captain, Californian, lifeboats). Then "we were wrong", the two
  new evidence sources by name, the thesis "it's physics", and an expert soundbite.
- **Answer withheld.** The mechanism is paid at 29:02 (67 %). Act one rewinds a year and runs chronologically.
- **Plant and payoff.** "Four compartments. Remember this. That number is going to matter later" (4:30) pays at
  15:30 (five breached). "We'll come back to that" (7:30, binoculars) pays at 33:00. "Lights still on" is planted
  at 16:00 and 22:30, and pays at 40:00 with the engineers who kept the boilers running.
- **Dramatic irony.** "That single decision saved 705 lives. Neither man knew it yet" (8:50).
- **Myth-busting micro-reveals.** These come every one to two minutes ("a critical detail most people miss"), so
  the title's promise is paid in small coins all the way through.
- **Mechanism by analogy.** Mechanism is explained through a familiar analogy (a road mirage, then its opposite),
  then closed with one causal-chain line: pressure, clear sky, inversion, bent light, false horizon, hidden berg.
  "The very conditions that made everyone feel safe were the conditions that killed them."
- **One mechanism, two mysteries.** The same mirage explains why the Californian misjudged Titanic's distance.
  It recasts a man blamed for a century; Murdoch's name is cleared by the 2022 scan.
- **Late new-evidence act.** A late act brings fresh evidence (the 2022 Magellan scan, 37:20 on) to carry the
  tail, and its peak is the most emotional beat (comments: "chills", "sobbing" at 40:27).
- **Human vignettes in the sinking act.** These sit inside the act itself (Astor, Guggenheim, the priest, the
  Lebanese dabke) and draw comments, not only at the end.
