RANKED CAUSES FOR 55–60 IMPRESSIONS vs. 173k–196k VIEWS
1. **Surfacing, not packaging, is the actual failure.** Impressions (not CTR) is the measured metric — 55–60 impressions means YouTube barely showed the thumbnail to anyone; a title/thumbnail defect changes CTR on impressions already granted, it can't manufacture the missing impressions. EarthDesk's own channel median is 188 views (per top12.json mult 1039x/918x) — these two wins are outliers even for them, meaning the algorithm's decision to surface is largely independent of subscriber base and was made per-video, not earned by channel authority we lack.
2. **Keyword-literal packaging is the biggest controllable gap.** Every competitor thumbnail/title puts "Anak Krakatau"/"Krakatoa"/"erupts" as the dominant on-frame text (search/suggested keyword match). Both our thumbnails omit the volcano name entirely ("THE IMPOSSIBLE TSUNAMI", "6+ ERUPTIONS IN A WEEK") — a viewer or the algorithm scanning for "Krakatoa" search/suggested-panel matches gets nothing to match against.
3. **Format mismatch to the news moment.** Competitors ran rolling breaking-news updates (no chapters, no CTA, present-tense cold opens) matching exactly what a "Krakatoa eruption" search-intent viewer wants right now. We ran retention-engineered mini-documentaries (siren mechanics, 1883 history-first, Alaska analog) — a different genre answering a different intent, launched under the same keyword.
4. New/zero-history channel (4 subs, first Krakatau upload ever) has no watch-history signal for YouTube to match against — compounds #1 but doesn't fully explain it since EarthDesk's low-median channel still broke out; channel newness lowers the ceiling, keyword/format mismatch explains why we didn't even clear the floor.
