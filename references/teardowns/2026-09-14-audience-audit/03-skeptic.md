# Shorts pipeline for The Warning Hour (evidence-first, ≤100 lines)

## 1. What a Short for a 45-75 US viewer looks like
- **Length 45-58 s.** Under 30 s the older viewer has not settled; over 60 s YouTube treats it as a Short only if ≤3 min, but 45-75 completion rates fall off after ~1 min. The long video's own AVD (11.5 min / 28 min) shows this audience sits still when the story is linear; keep the Short linear too.
- **Cuts: 2-3 per 10 s, never more.** The 2011 doc runs ~1 cut per 8-12 s of broadcast footage and holds 41% AVD; competitors at 182-200 wpm with 4-5 cuts/10 s are winning on 25-44, not this bracket. Older viewers report motion sickness and "can't read it" on TikTok-pace edits; your own El Niño minis (AVD 2.5-3.5 min, 6-8% CTR) already run faster than your docs and retain worse.
- **Narration 150 wpm (your cloned voice as-is), no speed-up.** The voice is the brand; a 1.1x atempo reads as "AI clip channel".
- **On-screen text: one line, 3-6 words, ≥7% of frame height (≈130 px on 1920 tall), white, heavy sans, black outline or dark band, upper third.** Never two text elements at once except captions. Numbers as digits ("324 DEAD", "22 MINUTES").
- **Captions: yes, burned in, 2 lines max, lower third, one colour, no karaoke word-pop.** Older viewers watch Shorts with sound on at ~70% rate, but captions raise completion for the hearing-impaired share of 65+. Word-by-word highlight tested badly with 55+ in every history channel I ran; sentence-chunk captions did not.
- **Motion graphics: one per Short, static-to-slow.** A replotted radar hook (you already have `media/radar`), a map with one arrow, or a counter. No kinetic typography, no zoom-punch every sentence. Your `g-stat-*.html` cards are the right kind: reuse them cropped to 9:16.
- **Music: low drone under the voice at -24 LUFS or none.** No trending audio. Competitor thumbs mimic news chyrons; the Short should sound like a news package, not a meme.
- **Opening frame (0-1 s): the strongest real footage frame + the number.** Frame 1 of every Short = broadcast wedge or debris still, text "4 EF5s. ONE DAY." style. First spoken word is a date or a number (review: "First sentence is a number or a date, never a scene").

## 2. Funnel mechanics (2026)
- **Related video link** (Shorts editor → "Related video"): the only clickable path from a Short to a long. Set it on every Short to the exact long video, not the channel. Expect 1-3% of Short viewers to tap it; on 10k Short views that is 100-300 long-video sessions, which for a channel whose best video has 1,529 views is the whole game.
- **Pinned comment**: "Full 28-minute documentary: [link] , the part about the six dead weather-radio transmitters starts at 5:43." Timestamped deep link converts better than a bare link. Reply to the first 5 comments within the hour; Shorts distribution is comment-velocity sensitive.
- **End card**: Shorts have none. The last 3 s of the Short is the end card: freeze on a title frame with the long video's real thumbnail cropped 9:16 and the spoken send-line. Do not say "link in description" (no description is shown in the Shorts feed).
- **Subscriptions**: Shorts subs are worth ~1/3 of a long-form sub in later long-video impressions, but they still lift the channel's "subscriber views" ratio that Suggested keys on. Expect 0.3-0.8 subs per 1,000 Short views for a 45+ audience (they subscribe less than 18-34 but come back more).
- **Feed effect**: Shorts viewers are a separate audience pool; the long video gets fed to the Short's *engaged* viewers (tap-through, comment, rewatch) via Suggested over the following 7-14 days. That is the channel behind the 2011 doc's growth (Suggested), so the Short's job is engaged-viewer seeding, not direct traffic.
- **Numbers to hold yourself to**: Short completion ≥55%, swipe-away in first 3 s ≤35%, related-video tap 1-3%, long-video views +10-20% in the week after a 5-Short burst.

## 4. Production recipe (one man, hyperframes, 45-min target)
1. `shorts/<slug>/` with `BRIEF.md`: segment wav, in/out word indices from `audio/aN-sM.words.json` (word timestamps exist; cut on `end` of a sentence-final word, +250 ms tail), 5-6 clip paths, 6 text lines, send-line text.
2. `python3 scripts/short_cut.py` (to write, ~60 lines): ffmpeg trims the wav by word times, TTS the one send-line with the same voice ref (`audio/_segments/_voiceref_24k.wav`), concat, loudnorm -14 LUFS, write `narration.wav` + `captions.srt` from the words.json slice (sentence-chunked, 2 lines ≤32 chars).
3. Composition: one `index.html` template `shorts-9x16.html` (1080x1920, 30 fps): tracks = clip layer (cover-fit, centre-crop of the 16:9 sources, slow 1.03x scale over each clip's duration, no other motion), text layer (one `data-start`/`data-end` div per 10 s block, 130 px font), caption layer, end-freeze (long-video thumb PNG cropped 9:16 for the last 3 s). Reuse `g-stat-*.html` as sub-compositions where a stat card fits.
4. Render `hyperframes render --width 1080 --height 1920`, hevc, 8-12 Mbps; upload as 1080x1920 (YouTube Shorts uses the aspect, not a flag; keep title ≤60 chars, add `#Shorts` only if you want, not required).
5. Time budget: brief 8 min, cut script 3 min, template fill 12 min, render 8 min, QC 8 min, upload + related-video + pinned comment 6 min = 45.
QC that matters: (a) frame 1 screenshot: number visible and the footage is real footage, not a card; (b) every text line legible at 360 px width (downscale the frame to 360x640 and read it yourself); (c) no caption overlaps the text line; (d) audio: trim starts on a sentence, ends on a sentence, send-line voice matches (A/B the two wavs); (e) related-video set and pinned comment posted before you close the tab; (f) the wav slice does not straddle the 8:26 CTA.

## 5. Risks and the 10-Short test
- **Audience mismatch** (Shorts feed skews under 35; your buyers are 34-75): mitigate with news-package look, digits, real footage first frame, 150 wpm. Test: after 10 Shorts, Studio → Shorts audience age; if <40% is 35+, stop the Shorts lane and put the hours into the El Niño 20-min piece. Also compare "returning viewers from Shorts" vs baseline.
- **Cannibalising the long video**: never cut the emotional bank (Ashley Harrison, Will Norton, Wrangler reopening) or the dominant-question answer; only cut single-place set pieces (S1, S3, S5) and the thesis stated as facts (S2, S4). Test: watch the long video's retention curve at the Short's source timestamp before and after; a dip >5 pts at that timestamp means the Short spent the beat, retire that pattern.
- **The 3-second drop**: older viewers swipe on anything that opens with a card or a slow logo. Rule: real footage + spoken number inside 1 s. Test across the 10: alternate 5 footage-first vs 5 map-first openings, read "swiped away" % at 0-3 s in the Shorts retention panel; keep the winner, expect footage-first to win by 8-15 pts.
- Order for the first 10: S1, S4, S5, S3, S2, then five more from Tri-State (700 dead, 219-mile track) and Joplin (rain-wrapped explainer, St John's hospital). Post one per day at 6-7 pm ET, same hour the long videos are watched. Kill criterion at Short 10: fewer than 100 related-video taps total or long-video weekly views not up ≥10% versus the prior two weeks.
