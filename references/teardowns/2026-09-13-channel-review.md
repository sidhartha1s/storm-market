## Competitor voice (6 transcripts, 3.4–4.3k words, 182–200 wpm)
- First sentence is a number or a date, never a scene. Promise in direct address inside 45 s ("By the end of this, you
  will understand…"), ~6–9 concrete numbers in the first 45 s.
- Long mechanism sentence then a 2–6 word kicker; median sentence 12–15 words; present tense for ongoing threat.
- Rhetorical questions as section headers (3–5 per video), "So why did…", "Now, here is where…". Hedges always name a
  source ("the USGS was careful to say"). Biggest reveal at 55–65 %. No recap. No spoken like/subscribe; endings are
  ominous present-tense ("…is still erupting."), plus "we'll be tracking this".
- AI tells they carry anyway: reveal-flip 15–20×/6 scripts, drama-stack cold opens 5/6, "here's what" 8+. The two
  lowest performers (St Helens 152k, Krakatau 61k) are the two without direct address.
- Thumbnails: caps text 2–4 words ("IT HAS STARTED", "6 DAYS TO IMPACT", "EAST COAST WARNING!"), red arrow or
  epicentre burst, place names on a map, news-chyron mimicry; no faces. Ours lack the map/arrow/authority cue; the
  Krakatau #3 thumb has no place, no map, no arrow.
