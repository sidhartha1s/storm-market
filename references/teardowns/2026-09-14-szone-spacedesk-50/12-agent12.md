**4. What to copy, what to do better.**

Copy the way they open with a real agency and a real number before anything else, and the way SpaceDesk flat out says "this is a forecast, not a measurement of the winter itself." That's honest, and I respect it. Copy naming the county, the office, the exact quote from NWS or NOAA instead of talking in generalities.

Do better than them on getting the dates right, every single time, because that one slip cost Szone more trust in the comments than any volcano conspiracy did. Skip the recap that repeats the whole video a second time near the end, just say it once and stop. Drop the reveal language, "here's the thing," "here's what," the fake cliffhangers before commercial. I can hear the seams. And never let one voice cover every disaster on earth in the same week. Pick a lane and stay in it, the way a real weatherman does.

Eight to ten minutes, no longer. I'm not sitting through twenty minutes of atmosphere-class lecture every morning.
