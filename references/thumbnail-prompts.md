# thumbnail-prompts.md — fill-in prompts for a thumbnail with Paul on it

Rules and the no-face test: `references/packaging-rules.md` "Paul on the thumbnail". This
file holds only the prompt text, and it is the one home of each system's colours, typefaces and positions. The
news lane's no-face prompts of 16 Sep 26 are a separate set: `ops/wave2/thumbnail-prompts-16sep.md`. Fill every `<SLOT>`, change nothing else: the fixed wording is what kept Paul on
his sheet and every word spelled right in the demos of 8 Oct 26. A slot's "e.g." shows what filled it in a demo;
the same slot name used twice takes the same text. Words Paul or the headline must show go in single quotes.

Every prompt attaches two images and names them in the text:
- **img1** is always `assets/paul/paul-sheet-v2.jpg` (Paul's character sheet).
- **img2** is one real photograph, frame or official map of the event. Never an AI storm scene.

## Field Notes (Paul beside a hand-drawn diagram on cream paper)

```
a YouTube thumbnail, 16:9 landscape, 1920x1080, for a <EVENT TYPE, e.g. tornado> documentary. Two reference images are attached. img1 is the character sheet of our presenter, Paul. img2 is <WHAT IMG2 IS, e.g. the official National Weather Service map of the El Reno tornado track>. HOW TO USE img1: Paul must be the same man as on the sheet: same face shape, same salt-and-pepper curly hair, same short grey stubble beard, same warm brown skin, mid-fifties. Do not make him younger or smoother. Use the outfit from the tile labelled 'Navy Jacket Look': navy jacket over a light blue shirt. Use the expression from the tile labelled 'Serious'. HOW TO USE img2: <WHAT TO REDRAW FROM IMG2 AND WHAT TO LEAVE OUT, e.g. redraw only the white crescent-shaped track outline from img2 as a hand drawing; do not copy its photo, its text boxes or its logos>. LAYOUT: Background is warm cream graph paper (#F2EAD8) with a faint pale grid, flat and evenly lit, covering the whole frame. Paul stands on the right third as a clean photographic cut-out, head and shoulders, his face about one quarter of the frame width, looking straight at the camera, soft even studio light, no glow, no outline, no drop shadow. HEADLINE, top-left, two lines, left aligned, in the typeface Anton (heavy condensed sans, all caps): line 1 reads '<LINE 1>' in near-black (#161616) with a slightly worn letterpress edge; line 2 reads '<LINE 2>' in cream (#F2EAD8) inside a rough red (#C8202A) dry-brush paint stroke box. The headline block is about 45 percent of the frame width and each line is about 22 percent of the frame height. DIAGRAM, bottom-left under the headline: <THE ONE DRAWING, in soft black pencil, e.g. the crescent track from img2 with a light grey pencil hatch inside it and a small dot at each end; or a hand-drawn timeline, a straight line with an arrow head and two dots>, with two or three labels in a neat architect handwriting (typeface like Architects Daughter): <EACH LABEL IN SINGLE QUOTES WITH ITS POSITION, e.g. '6:03 PM' at the left end, '6:43 PM' at the right end, and 'EL RENO, OK' under the track>. One red marker circle around <THE ONE DETAIL THE VIDEO IS ABOUT, e.g. the widest part of the track>. Nothing else: no logo, no badge, no border, no extra words. Spell every word exactly as given. Colours used: cream, black, red, plus Paul's natural colours. It must read clearly at 320 pixels wide.
```

## On the Ground (Paul stands inside the real photograph and points)

```
a YouTube thumbnail, 16:9 landscape, 1920x1080, for a <EVENT TYPE> documentary. Two reference images are attached. img1 is the character sheet of our presenter, Paul. img2 is <WHAT IMG2 IS, e.g. a real photograph of the El Reno tornado of 2013>. HOW TO USE img1: Paul must be the same man as on the sheet: same face shape, same salt-and-pepper curly hair, same short grey stubble beard, same warm brown skin, mid-fifties. Do not make him younger or smoother. Use the outfit from the tile labelled 'Navy Jacket Look': navy jacket over a light blue shirt. HOW TO USE img2: this real photograph is the scene. Keep <THE SUBJECT, e.g. the storm, the green field and the road> exactly as photographed; do not repaint the sky, do not add a funnel, lightning or debris. Crop it to 16:9. Keep its natural colour, only a little more contrast. LAYOUT: Paul stands inside the photograph <WHERE HE STANDS, e.g. on the road shoulder in the lower right>, waist up, his face about 15 percent of the frame width. His body is turned toward <WHAT HE POINTS AT, e.g. the storm>; he looks back over his shoulder at the camera with a grave, tight-lipped look and points at <WHAT HE POINTS AT> with his <LEFT OR RIGHT> arm fully stretched. He is lit by the same light as the scene, and his feet are out of frame. He must look photographed in that place, not pasted: matching grain, matching colour, a soft contact shadow. HEADLINE, top-left, two lines, left aligned, typeface Anton (heavy condensed sans, all caps), white (#FFFFFF) with a thick black outline and a soft black drop shadow: line 1 reads '<LINE 1>', line 2 reads '<LINE 2>'. Each line is about 20 percent of the frame height. ONE ARROW: a bold red (#E3212B) curved arrow with a thin white edge runs from the end of the headline to <WHERE THE ARROW LANDS>. SMALL TAG, bottom-left: '<PLACE YEAR>' in white Anton caps on a solid red (#E3212B) bar, about 6 percent of the frame height. Nothing else: no logo, no badge, no border, no extra words. Spell every word exactly as given. It must read clearly at 320 pixels wide.
```
