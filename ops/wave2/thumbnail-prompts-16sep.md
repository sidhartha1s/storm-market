# Thumbnail prompts, news lane (16 Sep 26)

Style: news card. Real photo / agency plate background, dark and high-contrast; one giant number or two-word noun; thin red date pill top-left; no logo, no face. Number: heavy high-contrast serif (Abril Fatface look), red #D0121F, black outer glow. Caption: condensed heavy sans (Anton / Bebas Neue look), off-white #F4F0E8, all caps. At 1280×720: number 260–300 px tall, caption 80–90 px, pill 28 px. Text in the left 60 %, subject in the right 40 %. Spell-check at 300 px. Three words max.

Common tail for every prompt:
"YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, 1280×720, photoreal, cinematic contrast, slight film grain, no watermark, no logo, no extra text beyond what is specified, letters crisp and correctly spelled."
