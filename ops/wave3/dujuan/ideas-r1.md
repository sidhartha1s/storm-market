## 1. A Depression Near Guam Is Aiming at Japan's Big Holiday
**Thumbnail:** "TARGETS JAPAN" over a JTWC track map / GOES satellite frame.
**Publish window:** 16-17 Sep, now (pre-name, TD stage).
**Hook:** "Three hundred miles east of Guam tonight, a tropical depression is spinning toward the biggest travel week of Japan's year."
**Why a 40+ American clicks:** Guam is a US territory with a US Navy/Air Force presence; this is the opening chapter of a storm that will threaten American servicemembers and travelers in Japan within the week.
**Why a Japanese viewer watches:** first alert ahead of Silver Week travel planning.
**Named quote:** JTWC Warning #04 (16/0900Z): "277 nm ENE of Guam... max sustained 30 kt, gusts 40 kt."
**Footage plan:** NOAA GOES/Himawari satellite loop; JTWC forecast track graphic; US Navy/USFJ Guam DVIDS b-roll; JMA depression bulletin graphic; our own map (Marianas to Japan).
**Kill number:** the system strengthens past 30 kt (JTWC) or JMA issues its first tropical-storm-strength forecast. No strengthening bulletin, no video.
