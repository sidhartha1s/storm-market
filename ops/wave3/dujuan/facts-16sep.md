**Honest US Saffir-Simpson comparison**: JMA reports **10-minute sustained** winds; the US NHC/Saffir-Simpson
scale uses **1-minute sustained** winds, which run roughly 12% higher for the same storm (standard WMO
conversion factor ≈0.88 for 10-min→1-min). A JMA "Very Strong" typhoon (44–54 m/s 10-min ≈ 85–105 kt 10-min)
converts to roughly 96–119 kt 1-min — i.e., **Category 2–3 hurricane range** on Saffir-Simpson — but this is a
statistical approximation, not a direct equivalence; do not present JMA and Saffir-Simpson category numbers as
interchangeable without this caveat. (Conversion factor is general meteorological convention, not
independently re-verified against a cited source this session — flag as reasoning, not a quoted fact.)
