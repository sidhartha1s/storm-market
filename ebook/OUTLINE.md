# The Storm-Ready Home — Phase 1 Outline

Date: 2026-07-26 · Status: awaiting owner sign-off (gates all prose)
Design contract: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-25-ebook-funnel-design.md`

**Counted total: 130 protections.** That is the enumerated number, not a rounded one. The sales page
uses 130 only if the outline survives review at 130 — if chapters merge, the number drops and the
page follows it.

## Per-protection template (every one of the 130 gets all seven fields in prose)

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Number + name | Global numbering, stable forever — hazard chapters cite by number |
| What it prevents | One line, concrete, names the failure mode |
| Cost | Real 2026 dollar band, sourced to a retailer at build time |
| Time | Minutes / one evening / one weekend / hire-the-heavy-part |
| Materials | Specific enough to buy without a second search |
| **What it does NOT do** | The honesty line. Non-negotiable — this is the trust mechanism |
| Region priority | Which of the 5 regions puts it in their first ten |

## Part B — The 60-day sequence

Not all 130 are scheduled. The sequence carries the **45 highest-return protections**; the rest are
reference, taken as budget allows. This is Yoder's explicit posture — pick what fits your house,
skip what doesn't — and it is what stops the book from reading as homework.

| Week | Theme | Protections |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Free and instant** — nothing bought | 85, 86, 88, 95, 109, 43 |
| 2 | **Water** | 1, 2, 3, 4, 7 |
| 3 | **Food and cold chain** | 13, 15, 18, 22 |
| 4 | **The paper week** — documents, inventory, insurance | 96, 97, 100, 102 |
| 5 | **Power and light** | 37, 38, 46, 47, 48 |
| 6 | **Shelter and the safe room** | 23, 25, 35, 106 |
| 7 | **Air and medical** | 49, 50, 52, 57, 58, 59 |
| 8 | **Structure — the weekend builds** | Region-dependent: from 69, 73, 74, 77, 78, 82, 83 |
| Close | **The drill** | Run the plan with the family. Two scenarios, timed. |

Weekdays are 15–40 minute tasks. Weekends carry anything requiring a store trip or a ladder.
Week 4 is deliberately placed before the spending weeks — it is the cheapest week and the one that
recovers the most money, which is what proves the book to a skeptical buyer early.

## Part D — Hazard chapter structure (all 23, uniform)

Each playbook runs the same five beats so the book is navigable under stress:

1. **The event** — one real, cited disaster, converted from channel research (NWS Service
   Assessment, NIST report, day-after archive). Not a story for its own sake: the forensic detail
   that explains the protections that follow.
2. **What actually kills and what actually costs** — mechanism, not drama.
3. **Before** — the protections that matter, cited by number in priority order.
4. **During** — the decision tree, in the order decisions arrive.
5. **After** — including the demoted household sub-points where they belong:
   - post-earthquake fire and gas
   - post-freeze pipe burst
   - post-rain sewer backup
   - post-outage CO
   - post-wildfire burn-scar debris flow
   - floodwater contamination and food loss
