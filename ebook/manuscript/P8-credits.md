## What a figure is allowed to say

A number printed inside a figure is never invented for the sake of a nicer picture. Every distance,
size, and time boundary shown in these thirteen diagrams is a fact already stated and sourced in the
chapter the figure sits in — the flood-opening dimensions, the defensible-space distances, the hail
sizes, the plume and ingestion radii, the six-phase clock. Where this book could not source a number,
the figure was drawn without it, the same rule the prose follows. The citation for each fact lives
where citations for every other fact in this book live: the **Sources** chapter, grouped by the
chapter the claim appears in, not repeated here.

## What is not in this book

No agency emblem, seal, logo, or copyrighted photograph appears anywhere in these pages. Where a
figure illustrates a fact published by a federal agency — the NRC's planning zones, the NWS damage-
threat tiers, the FEMA floodplain regulation, IBHS's tested distances — the underlying **fact** is
theirs and is cited; the **drawing** is this book's own, and no agency named in this book has drawn,
reviewed, or endorsed it.
