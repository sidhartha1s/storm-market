# Part Six — The Binder

Target: 80 pages · draft 1, 2026-07-29

Fillable pages. This is the part of the book the reader writes in, and it is the reason the book is bought
rather than borrowed.

---

## How the binder works

**A plan in your head is not a plan.** Every form here exists because something in Parts One to Five requires
a decision, and a decision that is not written down does not survive the moment it is needed.

**Print it or write in it.** Both are supported: the pages are laid out to be written on in the book, and the
same pages are available as a printable set so you can put them in a real ring binder, on the fridge, in the
go-bag or in the car.

**Fill it in over the sixty days**, not in one sitting. Part Seven names the form that belongs to each week.

**Then keep it somewhere obvious**, tell everyone where, and photograph the completed pages into the document
vault (Protection 96) — because a binder that burns with the house was a nice exercise.

**Update it once a year** with Protection 130.

---

## The forms

Forty-one forms across seven sections. Each is listed with its purpose, the protection it serves, and its
fields.

## Section A — The household (10 pages)

### Form 1 · Household roster
*Serves 96, 112. One page, one row per person.*

Name · date of birth · mobile · email · workplace or school with address and phone · blood type if known ·
notes.

### Form 2 · Medical summary, one per person
*Serves 57, 59, 68, and Part Four. Four pages — one per person, plus spares.*

Name and date of birth · conditions · **medications with doses and prescribing clinician** · allergies and
reactions · recent surgeries · implants and devices with model and serial · mobility and communication needs ·
vision and hearing needs · pharmacy name and phone · insurance carrier, policy and group number · clinician
names and phones · what helps in an emergency · what makes things worse.

> This is the page a stranger providing care needs. Copy it into the document envelope and the go-bag.

### Form 3 · Animals
*Serves 109.*

Name · species and breed · description and markings · microchip number **and the registry the chip is
registered with** · vaccination status and date · medications · vet name and phone · carrier location · the
three phone numbers: pet-friendly shelter, boarding facility, out-of-area contact who would take them.

### Form 4 · The contact sheet
*Serves 112. Designed to be photographed and printed wallet-size.*

**Out-of-area contact:** name, relationship, all numbers.
**Two local emergency contacts** who are not household members.
**Neighbours**, three, with a note of who is elderly, alone, disabled or medically dependent.
**Utilities:** electricity, gas, water, phone — with outage reporting numbers.
**Services:** insurance claims line and policy number, plumber, electrician, roofer, arborist, pharmacy,
vet, school office.
**Your county's ten-digit emergency backup number** for when 911 is down.
**Crisis lines:** 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — call or text), and the current Disaster Distress
Helpline number and text option, copied from SAMHSA's page on the day you fill this in.

### Form 5 · Who does what
*Serves 108, and the reunification agreement in Part Four.*

Per adult: assigned area for the thirty-minute list · assigned animal · assigned child · assigned neighbour
to check · assigned utility to shut off.

### Form 6 · The two meeting points
*Serves Part Four, Chapter Two.*

**Near home:** the specific place, described so a child could find it.
**Out of area:** the named place, with address and route.
Then the signed line: **nobody goes back for anybody.**

## Section B — The trigger plan (6 pages)

### Form 7 · The trigger plan
*Serves 95. The single most important page in the binder.*

One row per hazard that applies to you. Each row completes the sentence:

> **When _____ happens, we _____, without further discussion.**

Rows for: tornado watch · tornado warning · hurricane watch · evacuation order or warning for our zone ·
flash flood warning · wildfire evacuation warning · Red Flag Warning · winter storm warning · hard freeze
warning · extreme heat warning · earthquake felt near the coast · hazmat shelter order · power out for
more than ___ hours · indoor temperature above ___ or below ___.

Then a signature line for every adult, and a date.

### Form 8 · Region priority worksheet
*Serves the front matter's region table.*

Your region or regions · your ten protections in order · a done column with dates.

### Form 9 · The vulnerability walk
*Serves 78, 79, 128, and Part Four.*

Completed once, outdoors, during heavy rain and then again in dry weather. Where does water pond? Where does
it run against the house? Which drain backs up? Which window well fills? What is stored on the floor in the
garage? What is within five feet of the building? Which trees can reach the house?

### Form 10 · Alert setup checklist
*Serves 85, 86, 87, 91, 94.*

Wireless emergency alerts on, per device · county system enrolled, per adult · weather radio programmed to
which county codes · evacuation zone letter or number · fire weather alerts · space weather alerts · school
notification system.

## Section C — The house (14 pages)

### Form 11 · Shut-offs
*Serves 35, 129. Designed to be photographed and stuck on the panel door.*

**Water main:** location, which way it turns, tool needed, photograph space.
**Gas meter:** location, tool needed, and the rule written out — *only if I smell gas, hear it, see damage,
or am told to.*
**Breaker panel:** location, main breaker position, and a labelled circuit list.
**Water heater:** gas or power shut-off, and the drain-off procedure.
**Sprinklers, pool, well pump, septic:** as applicable.

### Form 12 · The three rooms
*Serves 23, 26, 29.*

**Safe room:** which room, why, permanent contents checklist, route from each bedroom, drill time.
**Warm room:** which room, heat source, insulation done.
**Cooling room:** which room, cooling source, shading done.

### Form 13 · Building facts
*Serves Parts Two and Three.*

Year built · construction type · roof covering, age and rating · number of storeys · basement, crawl space or
slab · heating type and whether it needs electricity, and how much · water source: mains or well · sewer or
septic · panel amperage · flood zone · evacuation zone · elevation relative to the street.

### Form 14 · The contractor list
*Serves 69–84. The most financially valuable page in the binder.*

What to demand next time anyone is working on the property, by trade:

**Roofer:** sealed roof deck (69) · ring-shank renailing (70) · impact-rated covering (71) · hurricane straps
(72) · secondary water barrier · ember-resistant vents (76) · get the class certification **in writing** for
the insurance credit (104).
**Electrician:** whole-home surge protective device (46) · transfer switch (39) · elevate the panel (81).
**Plumber:** backwater valve (82) · pipe insulation (34) · label the main (35).
**HVAC:** elevate mechanicals (81) · filter capacity (49) · fresh air intake location.
**General:** flood openings (80) · seismic anchoring and strapping (83) · gas flex connectors (84) ·
garage door bracing (74) · opening protection (73).

Each row: requested · quoted · done · documentation filed.

### Form 15 · Structural protections progress
*Serves 69–84.*

Sixteen rows, one per structural protection, with columns for: applies to me · cost quoted · scheduled ·
done · paperwork in the vault · insurance notified.

### Form 16 · Maintenance calendar
*Serves 130.*

Monthly, seasonal and annual: smoke and CO alarm tests · gutters · chimney sweep · generator run under load ·
HVAC filter · water and food rotation · go-bag repack · battery pack top-up · inventory refresh · drill.

## Section D — Supplies (12 pages)

### Form 17 · Water sizing and store
*Serves 1, 2.*

People × gallons per day × days, plus animals, equals **your number**. Then a store log: container, volume,
date filled, rotation date.

### Form 18 · The pre-fill list
*Serves 3. Designed to be taped inside a cupboard door.*

In order: which tubs, which pots, which bottles, where the bladder is, and who does it.

### Form 19 · Water treatment card
*Serves 7, 8. Taped inside the cupboard.*

Bleach ratios for the volumes you actually use, written out · tablet dosing · the boil protocol with the
altitude adjustment · **the three notices and what each one means** · what boiling does not fix.

### Form 20 · The no-cook pantry
*Serves 13, 14.*

Fourteen-day list with quantities and expiry dates, then the deep-pantry additions.

### Form 21 · Special supplies
*Serves 19, 20, 59, 60, 61, 68.*

Infant formula and feeding · allergy and special diet · pet food and medication · prescription buffer status
per person · refrigerated medication plan · dialysis, oxygen or infusion plan with the named backup facility
and its phone number · spares: glasses, hearing aid batteries, mobility parts, catheters, ostomy supplies.

### Form 22 · Fridge and freezer log
*Serves 15. Used during an outage.*

Time power lost · fridge temperature readings with times · freezer readings · discard decisions with
photographs taken · estimated values for the claim.

### Form 23 · Go-bag contents, per person
*Serves 107. One page per person.*

Standard contents checklist, plus the person-specific additions, plus **the size and season check date**.

### Form 24 · Five-minute and thirty-minute lists
*Serves 108. Taped inside a cupboard door.*

**Five minutes:** people, pets, go-bags, medication, phones and chargers, document envelope, keys, wallets.
**Thirty minutes:** the actual items, where in the house each is, and who carries it.

## Section E — Power and equipment (8 pages)

### Form 25 · The power priority list
*Serves 37. The page that decides what you buy.*

One row per load, in priority order: what it is · watts · hours per day needed · runs on battery, generator
or neither · notes. Then the total, which is the figure to size against.

### Form 26 · Backup power inventory
*Serves 38, 39, 40, 41, 44, 117.*

Battery packs with capacity and last-charged date · power station with capacity and tested loads · generator
with make, wattage, fuel type, transfer switch fitted, last run under load, oil changed · fuel stored with
quantity, stabiliser and rotation date · solar with rated and **measured** output · vehicle inverter capacity.

### Form 27 · Medical device power plan
*Serves 42, 61.*

Device · manufacturer and model · watts · **battery run time, from the supplier** · charging time · the
utility medical registry enrolment date and reference · the named facility and its phone number · the transport
arrangement and who has agreed to it · the clinician's instruction if the device stops.

### Form 28 · Alarm inventory
*Serves 47, 48.*

Location · type: smoke, CO or combined · power: mains, battery or both · **manufacture date and expiry** ·
last tested · battery changed.

## Section F — Evacuation (12 pages)

### Form 29 · Route one
*Serves 106.* Turn-by-turn in words · distance · driving time in normal traffic · bottlenecks noted ·
low-water crossings · fuel stops · date last driven.

### Form 30 · Route two
*Serves 106.* Same fields. **Must not share a bottleneck with route one.**

### Form 31 · Destinations
*Serves 111.*

**First choice:** name, relationship, address, all phones, how many people and animals they confirmed, date
confirmed.
**Second choice:** hotel, town, phone, pet policy.
**Third choice:** how to find out which public shelters have opened, and which local ones are pet-friendly and
accessible.

### Form 32 · Transport assistance
*Serves 111, and Part Four.*

Who cannot drive or cannot easily move · county registry enrolment date and reference · **the named person who
has agreed to come**, with their phone · the equipment that must travel · the vehicle it fits in.

### Form 33 · School and childcare
*Serves 112, and Part Four.*

Per child: school · shelter-in-place or evacuate · where children are taken · **who is on the authorised
release list** and the date it was updated · identification required · notification system · end-of-day and
bus-journey arrangements.

### Form 34 · House shutdown checklist
*Serves 113. Taped by the door.*

Windows and doors closed · curtains down or clear of glass · furniture away from windows · gutters flushed
if there is time · garden furniture and door mats moved · gas cylinders out and away · water main if freezing
· electricity if flooding expected · **gas only if instructed or leaking** · exterior lights on · note left
if local practice asks.

### Form 35 · Return checklist
*Serves 116.*

Area officially open · structural look from outside · smell for gas before entering · assume lines live ·
nothing switched on that was wet · boots, gloves, sleeves, eye protection · photograph before touching ·
generators outdoors only · boil notice status · chainsaw competence honest answer.

## Section G — Money and claims (18 pages)

### Form 36 · The insurance audit
*Serves 102. The page that recovers the most money.*

Carrier · policy number · claims phone · agent and phone · renewal date.
**Dwelling limit**, and the estimated rebuild cost today.
**Contents limit**, and **replacement cost or actual cash value** — circled, because this is the biggest number
on the page.
**Deductibles:** standard · wind and hail · hurricane, including whether it is a percentage and what that
percentage equals in dollars.
**Roof settlement basis**, and the age at which the schedule starts depreciating.
**Loss of use:** limit and time period.
**Exclusions confirmed:** flood · sewer backup and whether the endorsement is bought · earthquake · mould cap
· ordinance and law coverage.
**Sub-limits:** jewellery · art · collections · instruments · electronics · food spoilage.
**Separate policies:** flood — carrier, policy, effective date, structure and contents limits · earthquake ·
umbrella.
Then the agent's answer to: *if my house were destroyed tomorrow, what would this policy not cover?*

### Form 37 · Mitigation credits
*Serves 104.*

Carrier's credit table requested on ___ and received ___ · each qualifying upgrade with the documentation
filed · inspection form completed by whom and when · credit applied on which renewal · premium before and
after.

### Form 38 · The gap worksheet
*Serves the money chapter.*

Your own numbers, not the book's averages:

Estimated rebuild cost · dwelling limit · **difference**.
Estimated contents value, from the inventory · contents limit · adjustment if actual cash value ·
**difference**.
Deductibles you would actually pay.
Months of loss-of-use you would need · months the policy pays · **difference**.
Then: what federal assistance realistically averages against what it maximally pays, and what that leaves.
Then the honest total, and the line: **which of these gaps am I choosing to accept, and which am I closing this
year?**

### Form 39 · Home inventory log
*Serves 100, 101. Six pages.*

Room · item · make and model · **serial number** · approximate purchase date · approximate cost · receipt
filed · photographed · in the video walkthrough.
Then the high-value schedule: item · appraised value and date · endorsement in place.

### Form 40 · Document vault index
*Serves 96, 97, 123.*

What is in the vault · where copy one, two and three are · who else has access · date last refreshed ·
**date a restore was last tested** · what is in the paper envelope · where the envelope is.

### Form 41 · The claim log
*Serves 105. Four pages, used during a claim.*

Event date and description · claim numbers per policy · adjuster names and phones.
Then a dated call log: date, time, who, what was said, reference number, what was promised, by when.
Then an expense log: date, item, amount, receipt filed, reimbursable under which policy.
Then a mitigation log: what was done to prevent further damage, when, by whom, cost, receipt.
Then a discard log: item, approximate value, photographed, released by whom and when.

## The one-page front sheet

The binder opens with a single page, filled in last, that answers the questions a household actually asks
under stress:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Our region and first ten** | Form 8 |
| **Our safe room / warm room / cooling room** | Form 12 |
| **Water main · gas · breaker** | Form 11 — locations written here too |
| **Our trigger sentences** | Form 7 |
| **Where we meet, near home and out of area** | Form 6 |
| **Our out-of-area contact** | Form 4 |
| **Who collects the children** | Form 33 |
| **Insurance claims line and policy number** | Form 36 |
| **Two routes out and where we are going** | Forms 29–31 |
| **Whose job is what** | Form 5 |
| **Binder completed on** | ____________ |
| **Reviewed on** | ____ · ____ · ____ · ____ |

Then, at the bottom, the sentence the whole book has been building toward, on a signature line:

> **We have agreed that nobody goes back for anybody. Everyone goes to the agreed place and waits.**
