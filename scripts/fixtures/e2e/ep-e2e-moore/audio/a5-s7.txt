Fourteen years later, the test came back. On May twentieth, 2013, an EF5 tornado went through Moore again. Afterward, the weather service sent a team to ask people what they did. Many said they'd heard about the storms at least twenty-four hours ahead, and most said they had time to take shelter. So they did. They went into the interior closets, bathrooms, safe rooms and cellars they'd picked out long before. The report says people followed plans they'd practiced, and survived in shelters chosen ahead of time.
