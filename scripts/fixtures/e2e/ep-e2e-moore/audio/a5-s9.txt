One survivor said people knew not to get in their cars. It should have kicked in the moment they touched the door handle. But the fear took over, especially when a weatherman they'd trusted all their lives told them to drive. On May thirty-first, every person the tornadoes took directly was in a vehicle, eight in all. Four of them were storm chasers. Two were a woman of twenty-six and a baby seventeen days old, in a vehicle parked along Interstate forty. Later, many people told the team they were ashamed they'd fled instead of sheltering.
